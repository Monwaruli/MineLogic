### Requirement 12: Embedded Video Walkthroughs

**User Story:** As a learner who prefers watching, I want a narrated video beside each tutorial that I can pause, rewind, and scrub, so that I have a second way to learn without a teacher.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Video_Panel SHALL accept, for each Difficulty independently, zero or one Video_Source that is either a direct video file URL (a video file that the latest stable Chrome, Edge, and Firefox can each play) or a YouTube embed URL, and SHALL treat an empty or whitespace-only value as no Video_Source.
2. WHILE the Tutorial_Player shows a Lesson to anyone other than a Group_B Participant in the Intermediate learning block, and the viewport is at least 1280 CSS pixels wide, THE Video_Panel SHALL appear for the Lesson's Difficulty side by side with the Tutorial_Player in one row, with neither component covering any part of the other and with no horizontal scrolling.
3. WHILE the Tutorial_Player shows a Lesson to anyone other than a Group_B Participant in the Intermediate learning block, and the viewport is narrower than 1280 CSS pixels, THE Video_Panel SHALL appear for the Lesson's Difficulty below the Tutorial_Player, with the Video_Panel's top edge at or below the Tutorial_Player's bottom edge.
4. WHEN the Video_Panel appears for a Difficulty that has a Video_Source, THE Video_Panel SHALL load that Difficulty's video paused at position 0:00, SHALL NOT start playback until the learner starts it, and SHALL provide, for both Video_Source types, a control that starts and pauses playback, a rewind control that moves the playback position back 10 seconds (to 0:00 when the position is under 10 seconds), and a scrub bar that shows the playback position and the video's duration and moves playback to any point from 0:00 to the end of the video that the learner selects, with every resulting position within 1 second of its target.
5. IF the Video_Panel is shown for a Difficulty that has no Video_Source, THEN THE Video_Panel SHALL show, in place of the video and with no playback controls, a placeholder stating that the video for that Difficulty is coming soon.
6. IF the video player reports a load or playback error, or the video is not ready to start playback 15 seconds after the Video_Panel starts loading it, THEN THE Video_Panel SHALL replace the video with an error message stating that the video could not be loaded and a retry control that loads the video again under the same 15-second limit, and SHALL leave the Tutorial_Player usable and the learner's Lesson progress unchanged.
7. WHERE a caption file is configured for a Video_Source, THE Video_Panel SHALL provide a captions control that switches between showing no captions and showing each caption cue's text from the cue's start time to its end time, within 1 second.
8. FOR ALL Lessons, the Tutorial_Player SHALL let the learner complete the Lesson without playing the video, and FOR ALL Video_Panel states (video loaded, placeholder, error message, or hidden) and all Video_Events interleaved with the learner's Player_Actions, the same Player_Actions SHALL produce the same Do_Step and Lesson completions (independence property).
9. WHEN playback starts, playback pauses before the end of the video (including when the learner leaves the Lessons of the video's Difficulty during playback, and excluding stalls while buffering), the playback position moves other than by playing (counting one move per rewind and one per scrub from press to release), or playback reaches the end of the video, THE Video_Panel SHALL emit exactly one Video_Event of the matching type (play, pause, seek, or ended), for both Video_Source types, with every reported position within 1 second of the actual playback position.
10. WHEN the learner moves to another Learn_Step, Do_Step, or Lesson of the same Difficulty, performs a Player_Action on a Mini_Board, or resizes the viewport across 1280 CSS pixels, THE Video_Panel SHALL keep a playing video playing with no pause and no jump in playback position of more than 1 second, keep a paused video paused within 1 second of the same position, and emit no Video_Event.
11. FOR ALL sequences of plays, pauses, seeks, and playing durations on one video, the Watched_Seconds count SHALL equal the number of distinct whole seconds that the played intervals overlap, so that replaying seconds already counted leaves the count unchanged and the count never exceeds the video's duration rounded up to a whole second (model and idempotence properties).
12. WHILE a Mastery_Checkpoint is shown, THE Video_Panel SHALL be hidden, including when the Mastery_Checkpoint shares a screen with the Tutorial_Player.
