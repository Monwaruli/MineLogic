### Requirement 15: Timed No-Guess Games

**User Story:** As a learner, I want full timed games at each difficulty on boards that never force a guess, so that I can test my skill without hints and without luck deciding the result.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN the learner, the Daily_Sweep, or the Study_Module opens a Timed_Game at a Difficulty, THE Timed_Game SHALL display the No_Guess_Board supplied by the Board_Provider, which is the game's Assigned_Fixed_Board if the game has one and otherwise a Board with that Difficulty's width, height, and mine count, with the game clock stopped at 0 until the Start_Tile's Reveal_Click is applied.
2. WHEN the Start_Tile's Reveal_Click is applied in a Timed_Game, THE Timed_Game SHALL start the game clock at 0, display it as the elapsed whole seconds rounded down (including values above 999), update the displayed value within 100 milliseconds after each whole second passes on the Reference_Device, and keep it on screen with the complete Board without scrolling at viewports of at least 1280 by 720 CSS pixels.
3. WHILE a Timed_Game is an Active_Game, THE Timed_Game SHALL include every period in which the browser tab is hidden or the browser window is minimized in the game clock and the Move_Log times, so that the clock shown within 100 milliseconds after the tab becomes visible again includes the hidden period, and SHALL offer no control that pauses the game clock.
4. WHILE a Timed_Game's Board is displayed and the game has not ended, THE Timed_Game SHALL display no Overlay, Nudge, Pattern_Type label, hint control, or other marker, highlight, or message that indicates whether a covered Tile is safe or a mine, other than the Start_Tile marker, the prompt that points to it, and the learner's own flags.
5. WHEN a Timed_Game ends, THE Timed_Game SHALL record the End_Entry's time as the game's elapsed time in whole milliseconds since the Start_Tile's Reveal_Click (the time of the winning or losing Reveal_Action for the outcomes Won and Lost, and the time the Study_Module sets for Timed_Out and Interrupted), and SHALL stop the game clock showing that time in whole seconds rounded down.
6. WHEN a Timed_Game other than a Pre_Test or Post_Test game ends, THE Timed_Game SHALL display, within 2 seconds of the game-ending action on the Reference_Device for every Difficulty, a summary of the outcome, the elapsed time in seconds with three decimal places, and every other Game_Metric, showing each Game_Metric that is not available as not available rather than as a number (including Chording_Efficiency for every outcome other than Won, while Total_Clicks and 3BV are still shown), and showing the partial mark with its excluded counts only when the Game_Metrics are partial.
7. THE Timed_Game SHALL display in the summary, beside each Game_Metric's value and readable without hovering or clicking, a one-sentence explanation of what the Game_Metric measures, with all values of a metric reported per Pattern_Type sharing one explanation, with the explanations of Logical_Move_Rate, Pattern_Recognition_Speed, and Chording_Efficiency stating whether a higher or a lower value is better (higher for Logical_Move_Rate, lower for the other two), and with the Chording_Efficiency explanation stating that it is reported only for won games.
8. WHEN a Timed_Game other than a Pre_Test or Post_Test game ends, THE Progress_Service SHALL store the result, consisting of the Game_Identifier, the Difficulty, the Game_Start_Time, the Board identifier, the Board's canonical Board_Format text, the Metrics_Version, the Game_Metrics, and the serialized Move_Log including the End_Entry, in the browser's local storage if the learner is a Guest when the game ends, or in the Backend through the Sync_Queue if the learner is signed in to an Account when the game ends.
9. IF the Board_Provider cannot supply a Timed_Game's Assigned_Fixed_Board, THEN THE Timed_Game SHALL show an error message stating that the Board could not be loaded, together with a retry control that requests the Assigned_Fixed_Board again, and SHALL never substitute a Board from the Board_Generator or the Puzzle_Bank.
10. WHILE a Timed_Game is an Active_Game, THE Timed_Game SHALL pass every Player_Action to the Board_Engine without holding, blocking, or intercepting it on the basis of the Solver's classification, so that the 50 millisecond display bound of Requirement 2.4 runs from the input and a Reveal_Action that reveals a mine ends the game as Lost.
11. IF the learner navigates to another MineLogic screen, reloads the page, or closes the page while a Timed_Game without an Assigned_Fixed_Board is an Active_Game, THEN THE Timed_Game SHALL discard the game, store no result, show no summary, and offer no way to resume the game.
12. FOR ALL ended Timed_Games, every outcome, elapsed time, and Game_Metric value shown on screen SHALL equal the corresponding value stored for that game, and the stored elapsed milliseconds SHALL equal the time of the stored Move_Log's End_Entry (result consistency property).
13. IF the Metrics_Engine returns an error instead of Game_Metrics for a Timed_Game other than a Pre_Test or Post_Test game, THEN THE Timed_Game SHALL display a summary with the outcome, the elapsed time, and a message that the metrics could not be computed, and SHALL store no result for that game.
