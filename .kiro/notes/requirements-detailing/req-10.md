### Requirement 10: Onboarding and Diagnostic Skill Check

**User Story:** As a new learner, I want a two-minute check of what I already know, so that MineLogic can show me where my logic breaks down and where to start.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN a Guest or an Account opens the Hub and that person's progress, once loaded, holds no Onboarding_Milestone, THE Onboarding_Flow SHALL present the two Background_Questions and then the three Skill_Check_Snippets one at a time in the order Beginner, Intermediate, Expert, show a Skip control on every Onboarding_Flow screen, and end by opening the Hub after the Expert snippet.
2. THE Onboarding_Flow SHALL ask about prior experience with exactly one choice among "new to Minesweeper", "casual player", and "regular player", ask about input device with exactly one choice among "mouse", "trackpad", and "other", and enable the Continue control that leads to the Beginner Skill_Check_Snippet only when both questions have a choice.
3. WHILE a Skill_Check_Snippet is shown and not yet answered, THE Skill_Check SHALL display the snippet's mine counter and no game clock, apply each Flag_Toggle, and ignore each Chord_Click.
4. WHEN the learner makes the first Reveal_Click on a Skill_Check_Snippet, THE Skill_Check SHALL take the clicked Tile as the learner's answer without revealing any Tile, record the result for the snippet's Difficulty as Correct if the answer is the Provably_Safe Tile, as Trap with the Trap_Tile's label if the answer is a Trap_Tile, and as Other in every remaining case, and ignore every later Player_Action on that snippet.
5. WHEN the learner answers a Skill_Check_Snippet, THE Skill_Check SHALL, within 100 milliseconds of the answer on the Reference_Device, mark the answered Tile, highlight the Provably_Safe Tile with an Overlay, display a message that states whether the answer is the Provably_Safe Tile, display the snippet's explanation of the deduction that proves that Tile safe, and show a Next control that moves to the step after the snippet.
6. WHEN the Skill_Check is complete, meaning all three Skill_Check_Snippets have an answer, THE Progress_Service SHALL store the three results as milestones together with an Onboarding_Milestone, so that each Mastery_Bar takes the value that Requirement 17 defines (for a learner with no other milestones, 10% for each Difficulty whose result is Correct and 0% for each other Difficulty), and SHALL keep all three Difficulties unlocked, meaning the Hub lets the learner open every loop step of every Difficulty whatever the results.
7. WHEN the learner selects the Skip control, THE Onboarding_Flow SHALL end, discard every answer given in it, store an Onboarding_Milestone and no Skill_Check result, and open the Hub with every Mastery_Bar unchanged (0% for a learner with no other milestones) and all three Difficulties unlocked.
8. THE Content_Validator SHALL verify that exactly one Skill_Check_Snippet exists for each Difficulty and that each snippet's initial Board_State is not an Inexact_State, has exactly one Provably_Safe covered Tile, and has at least one Trap_Tile, with every Trap_Tile listed in the snippet's metadata being a covered Tile that is not Provably_Safe.
9. THE Content_Validator SHALL verify that the Pattern_Detector attributes each Skill_Check_Snippet's Provably_Safe Tile, in the snippet's initial Board_State, to a Pattern_Type of the snippet's tier, with the Intermediate snippet attributed to Wall_1_2_1, and that the snippet's metadata, kept separate from its Board_Format text, holds a deduction explanation of 1 to 400 characters and, for each Trap_Tile, a label of 1 to 40 characters and an explanation of 1 to 400 characters.
10. WHILE a Study_Session is in progress in the current browser, from the recorded consent until the completion screen or a confirmed withdrawal, THE MineLogic client SHALL not present the Onboarding_Flow, so that no Participant sees a Skill_Check_Snippet before the Learning_Phase begins.
11. IF the learner closes, reloads, or navigates away from the Onboarding_Flow before it ends, THEN THE Onboarding_Flow SHALL keep the answers already given and, the next time it is presented in that browser, resume at the first Background_Question or Skill_Check_Snippet that has no answer.
12. IF the learner answers a Skill_Check_Snippet with a Trap_Tile, THEN THE Skill_Check SHALL display, beside the deduction explanation, that Trap_Tile's explanation of why it cannot be proven safe.
