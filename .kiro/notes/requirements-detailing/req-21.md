### Requirement 21: Study Entry, Consent, and Participant Identity

**User Story:** As the researcher, I want participants to join through a study link, give informed consent before anything is logged, and stay anonymous, so that the evaluation study is ethical and its data cannot identify anyone.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN a person opens a Study_Link in a browser that holds no Study_Identity, and the Study_Link's Study_Code exactly matches, letter case included, the Study_Code of an active Study_Config that passes every check of criterion 3, THE Study_Module SHALL show, as the first study screen and before storing any record, the Study_Config's consent text, each of its consent statements with its own affirmation control that starts unaffirmed, an agree control that is enabled only while every consent statement is affirmed, a decline control, and a control that opens the Resume_Code entry screen.
2. IF a person opens a Study_Link in a browser that holds no Study_Identity, and the Study_Link's Study_Code exactly matches no Study_Config's Study_Code or matches the Study_Code of a Study_Config that is not active, THEN THE Study_Module SHALL show, in place of the consent screen, a message that the study link is not valid and a control that opens the Resume_Code entry screen, and SHALL create no Participant and store no record.
3. IF a person opens a Study_Link in a browser that holds no Study_Identity, and the Study_Link's Study_Code matches an active Study_Config that has no consent text, no consent text version, or no consent statement, that has, for any of the five Evaluation_Boards, no Board or Board_Format text that the Board_Parser rejects, whose survey items break the rules of Requirement 24 criterion 10, or the Intermediate Difficulty has no Video_Source under Requirement 12, THEN THE Study_Module SHALL show, in place of the consent screen, a message that the study cannot start because its configuration is incomplete and a control that opens the Resume_Code entry screen, and SHALL create no Participant and store no record.
4. WHEN the person has affirmed every consent statement and selects the agree control, THE Study_Module SHALL create exactly one Participant, with a new Participant_ID and a new Resume_Code, by storing in the Backend a consent record that holds the Participant_ID, the Study_Code, the version identifier of the consent text shown, and the consent time, which is the Backend's UTC time of storage in ISO 8601 format with millisecond precision.
5. WHEN the person selects the decline control, THE Study_Module SHALL show a thank-you screen that offers no control leading to a Study_Step, create no Participant, and store no consent record, Study_Record, or Study_Identity, so that opening a Study_Link again in that browser shows the screen that criteria 1 to 3 define, with every consent statement unaffirmed.
6. FOR ALL Study_Record writes that reach the Backend, from any browser and in any order relative to consent and withdrawal, THE Backend SHALL store a write only if a consent record for the write's Participant_ID exists when the write arrives, and SHALL reject every other write without storing any part of it, so that every stored Study_Record has a consent record (consent-first property).
7. FOR ALL Study_Sessions, including those whose consent is given while the person is signed in to an Account or while the browser holds Guest progress, the consent record, every Study_Record, and the Study_Identity SHALL hold no email address, Account identifier, or Display_Name; no Account record or Guest progress SHALL hold a Participant_ID or Resume_Code; and signing in, signing out, or creating an Account SHALL leave the Participant_ID and Resume_Code unchanged (separation property).
8. WHEN the Backend confirms that the consent record is stored, THE Study_Module SHALL store the Study_Identity in the browser and open the Resume_Code display, which shows the Participant's Resume_Code, a statement asking the Participant to save the Resume_Code because it is needed to resume the Study_Session or to withdraw in another browser, and a Continue control that opens the Background_Questions.
9. WHEN a browser that holds the Study_Identity of a Participant who has not withdrawn opens any Study_Link or reloads a Study_Session screen, THE Study_Module SHALL open, whether or not the Participant's Study_Config is still active, the first Study_Step in Study_Step order that is not complete according to the Participant's records in the Backend and in that browser's Sync_Queue. FOR ALL sequences of Study_Step completions, page reloads, page closings, and Study_Link reopenings in one browser, no Study_Step SHALL be opened again after it is complete, and no Study_Step SHALL be opened while an earlier Study_Step is not complete (resume property).
10. WHEN a person submits, on the Resume_Code entry screen, text that, ignoring letter case, spaces, and hyphens, equals the Resume_Code of a Participant who has not withdrawn, THE Study_Module SHALL store that Participant's Study_Identity in the browser in place of any Study_Identity the browser held, and SHALL open, whether or not the Participant's Study_Config is still active, the first Study_Step in Study_Step order that is not complete according to the Participant's records in the Backend.
11. THE Study_Module SHALL show a Withdraw control on the screen of every Study_Step, from the Resume_Code display through the completion screen and including during Pre_Test and Post_Test games, and on the Resume_Code entry screen, where the control applies to the Participant whose Resume_Code is entered. The control SHALL open a confirmation that states that all of the Participant's study data will be deleted and cannot be restored, with a confirm control and a cancel control that closes the confirmation and changes nothing, without pausing the clock of a test game in progress.
12. WHEN a person selects the confirm control of the withdrawal confirmation, THE Backend SHALL delete, in one operation that either completes in full or changes nothing, the Participant's consent record and every Study_Record with the Participant's Participant_ID, so that the Participant's Resume_Code matches no Participant, no file that the Data_Exporter produces afterwards contains the Participant_ID, and criterion 6 rejects every later Study_Record write for that Participant_ID.
13. WHEN the Backend confirms the deletion of criterion 12, THE Study_Module SHALL end the Study_Session, remove from the browser the Participant's Study_Identity and every Study_Record of the Participant that is waiting in the Sync_Queue or kept as a local copy, and show, in place of the current screen and any game in progress, a confirmation that the Participant's study data has been deleted, with no control that leads to a Study_Step.
14. IF a person submits text on the Resume_Code entry screen, with the resume control or the Withdraw control, that, ignoring letter case, spaces, and hyphens, equals no Resume_Code of a Participant who has not withdrawn, or a browser that holds a Study_Identity opens a Study_Link or reloads a Study_Session screen after that Study_Identity's Participant has withdrawn, THEN THE Study_Module SHALL show a message that the Resume_Code is not valid, open no Study_Step, store no record, withdraw nothing, remove from the browser any Study_Identity of the withdrawn Participant together with that Participant's Study_Records waiting there, and leave any other Study_Identity in the browser unchanged.
15. IF the Backend does not answer within 10 seconds, or answers with a failure, when the Study_Module reads a Study_Config under criteria 1 to 3, stores a consent record under criterion 4, reads a Participant's records under criterion 9 or 10, checks a Resume_Code under criterion 10 or 14, or deletes records under criterion 12, THEN THE Study_Module SHALL show an error message that the study service could not be reached, with a retry control that repeats the same operation so that repeated consent submissions create at most one Participant, keep the current screen's entries, the browser's Study_Identity, and any Study_Session in progress unchanged, open no new Study_Step, and show neither the message of criterion 2 nor that of criterion 14.
16. THE Progress_Service SHALL store none of the activity on Study_Session screens (Background_Question answers, Skill_Check results, Lesson and Mastery_Checkpoint completions, Practice_Mode boards, and Pre_Test and Post_Test games) in the browser's Guest progress or in any Account's progress, so that this activity is stored only as Study_Records and changes no milestone, Mastery_Bar, game result, Bank_History, or leaderboard. Daily_Sweep games played during the Retention_Window are not activity on Study_Session screens.
