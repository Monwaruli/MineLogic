### Requirement 25: Anonymized Study Logging

**User Story:** As the researcher, I want detailed anonymized logs of each participant's games and learning activity, so that I can analyze outcomes and check that each group received its intended treatment.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN a Test_Game ends with any outcome (Won, Lost, Timed_Out, or Interrupted), or a Sweep_Attempt ends with the outcome Won or Lost under the conditions of Requirement 24 criterion 8, THE Study_Logger SHALL store exactly one Study_Record for that game with the Participant_ID; the phase (Pre_Test, Post_Test, or Retention_Window); the Difficulty; the outcome; the elapsed milliseconds, equal to the End_Entry's time; the Board identifier; the Board's canonical Board_Format text; the Metrics_Version; the Game_Metrics of the game's one Metrics_Engine run (Requirement 9 criterion 1), or the name of the error when that run returns an error; the serialized Move_Log including the End_Entry; for a Test_Game, the Time_Cap in milliseconds; and for a Sweep_Attempt, the Sweep_Day and the attempt number.
2. WHILE a Study_Session screen displays a Test_Game's Board, a Do_Step's Mini_Board, a Checkpoint_Variation, or a learning block's Practice_Mode board, WHEN the browser reports that the page has become hidden (because its tab is in the background, its window is minimized, or the page is being closed or reloaded) or has become visible again, THE Study_Logger SHALL record one Study_Record with the Participant_ID, the new visibility state (hidden or visible), the displayed Board's identifier, and, when that Board's game is an Active_Game, the game's elapsed whole milliseconds at the change, measured as in its Move_Log.
3. WHEN, in a Study_Session, the Skill_Check, a learning block, or a learning block's Lessons, Mastery_Checkpoint, or Practice_Mode part starts or ends, THE Study_Logger SHALL record one Study_Record with the Participant_ID, the item (the Skill_Check, or the block's Difficulty and, for a part, which of the three parts), whether the item started or ended, and, for an end, whether the item was complete or was ended by the block's Continue control. The Skill_Check starts when its first Skill_Check_Snippet is first displayed and ends when its third Skill_Check_Snippet is answered. A learning block starts when its first part is first displayed and ends as Requirement 23 criterion 1 defines. A part starts when its first screen is first displayed and ends when it becomes complete as Requirement 23 criterion 2 defines or, if it has started and is not complete, when the Participant selects the block's Continue control. A display of an item that the Participant's records already show as started SHALL record no new start, and an item that never starts SHALL have no record.
4. WHEN, in a Study_Session, a Checkpoint_Attempt presents a Checkpoint_Variation, the Participant clears a Checkpoint_Variation or makes a Slip on it, or a Checkpoint_Attempt is passed, THE Study_Logger SHALL record one Study_Record with the Participant_ID, the Difficulty, the Checkpoint_Attempt's number (one more than the number of the Participant's earlier recorded Checkpoint_Attempts at that Difficulty in the Study_Session), and the event's kind (presented, cleared, Slip, or passed), with the Checkpoint_Variation's identifier for every kind except passed and, for a Slip, the Slip's type (Guess or Wrong_Flag), the slipped Player_Action's type, and that Player_Action's Tile, so that each presented Checkpoint_Variation's result is cleared, Slip, or (when neither is recorded) unfinished, and the timestamp of the passed record is the pass time.
5. WHEN, on a learning block's Practice_Mode board, the Participant performs an Intercepted_Action or selects the More Help control, THE Study_Logger SHALL record one Study_Record with the Participant_ID, the Difficulty, the Board identifier, the event's kind (Intercepted_Action or More Help selection), the game's elapsed whole milliseconds at the input, measured as in its Move_Log, for an Intercepted_Action its action type (Reveal_Click or Chord_Click) and its clicked Tile, and the Nudge stage that the Hint_Coach displays once the event is handled: 1, 2, or none (for an Intercepted_Action in an Inexact_State).
6. WHEN, in a Study_Session, a Video_Appearance begins or ends, the Video_Panel emits a Video_Event, or the Video_Panel displays the error message of Requirement 12 criterion 6, THE Study_Logger SHALL record one Study_Record with the Participant_ID, the Difficulty, and the event's kind (appearance start, appearance end, play, pause, seek, ended, or error message shown), with, for a Video_Event, its playback position in seconds (for a seek, the positions before and after) and its Watched_Seconds count for the current Video_Appearance, and, for an appearance end, including one caused by closing or reloading the page, the Video_Appearance's final Watched_Seconds count.
7. WHEN a Participant selects the Continue control of the Background_Questions step, THE Study_Logger SHALL record exactly one Study_Record with the Participant_ID and the option chosen for each of the two Background_Questions, using the option texts of Requirement 10 criterion 2.
8. FOR ALL Study_Records, in the browser or in the Backend, including those created while the Participant is signed in to an Account or while the browser holds Guest_Progress, the record SHALL identify the Participant only by the Participant_ID and SHALL contain no email address, Account identifier, Display_Name, Resume_Code, Participant_Credential, or IP address, and no other value that MineLogic takes from an Account, the Auth_Service, or the browser's network connection, apart from text that the Participant typed into an open-ended Survey_Form prompt, which is stored exactly as typed (anonymity property).
9. THE Study_Logger SHALL give every Study_Record it creates one timestamp, the moment of the event that the record describes (for a game record, the moment the game ends, which for an Interrupted Test_Game is the moment the Study_Module ends it under Requirement 22 criterion 12), read from the Participant's computer clock and written in ISO 8601 UTC format with exactly three fractional-second digits and the Z designator, for example 2025-03-14T15:09:26.535Z.
10. THE Study_Logger SHALL send every Study_Record it creates to the Backend only as a Sync_Queue write for the Participant, never as a Progress_Record, adding the write to the Sync_Queue in the browser's local storage before displaying any screen that follows the recorded event (and before the page unloads when the event occurs while the page is being closed or reloaded), without making any Study_Session screen wait for the Backend to confirm a Study_Record. FOR ALL Study_Records that the Study_Logger creates in one browser for a Participant who has not withdrawn, while that browser's local storage accepts writes, and including when page reloads, page closings, browser restarts, Backend_Outages, and repeated deliveries of one write occur, once that browser's Sync_Queue holds no pending write for the Participant, the Backend SHALL hold each such record exactly once with every value unchanged, unless the Backend rejected it and the Sync_Queue dropped it under Requirement 20 criterion 13 (no-loss and no-duplicate property).
11. WHEN a Participant answers a Skill_Check_Snippet in a Study_Session, THE Study_Logger SHALL record one Study_Record with the Participant_ID, the snippet's Difficulty and identifier, the answered Tile, and the result that Requirement 10 criterion 4 defines (Correct, Trap with the Trap_Tile's label, or Other).
12. WHEN the Backend assigns a group to a Participant under Requirement 23 criterion 3, THE Backend SHALL store the group as a Study_Record of that Participant, with the Participant_ID and a timestamp of the Backend's UTC time of the assignment in the format of criterion 9, in the same operation as the assignment and without any Sync_Queue write, so that each Participant with a group has exactly one group record, a repeated assignment request stores no second one, no Participant session can insert or change a group record, and the withdrawal of Requirement 21 criterion 12 deletes it with the Participant's other Study_Records.
13. IF the Backend receives a Test_Game Study_Record write for a Participant_ID and an Evaluation_Board for which it already holds a Test_Game Study_Record, THEN THE Backend SHALL confirm the write without changing anything when it equals the stored record in every value, and SHALL otherwise reject the write, store no part of it, and keep the stored record unchanged. FOR ALL sequences of Test_Game Study_Record writes for one Participant_ID, including repeated Sync_Queue deliveries, writes from two browsers, and writes that arrive in a different order from the order in which their games ended, the Backend SHALL hold at most one Test_Game Study_Record for each of the five Evaluation_Boards, equal to the first such write it accepted (single-test-record property).
14. WHEN a Participant meets a Do_Step's Objective in a Study_Session, THE Study_Logger SHALL record one Study_Record with the Participant_ID, the Difficulty, the identifiers of the Do_Step and of its Lesson, and whether meeting the Objective completes the Lesson under Requirement 11 criterion 9, so that Lesson completions, the resume point of Requirement 23 criterion 13, and the incomplete Lessons of Requirement 23 criterion 10 can be judged from the Participant's Study_Records alone.
