### Requirement 14: Follow-Along Practice with the Two-Stage Nudge

**User Story:** As a learner, I want untimed practice that stops me before I guess and points me toward the numbers to compare without giving away the answer, so that I learn to find safe tiles myself.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN the learner starts Practice_Mode at a Difficulty, THE Practice_Mode SHALL present a No_Guess_Board of that Difficulty supplied by the Board_Provider, with no game clock and no time limit.
2. WHEN the learner performs the Reveal_Click on the Start_Tile or a Logical_Move, THE Practice_Mode SHALL apply the action and remove any displayed Nudge together with its More Help control.
3. IF the learner performs an Intercepted_Action, THEN THE Practice_Mode SHALL leave the action unapplied and, within 100 milliseconds of the input on the Reference_Device, display an interception message that stays until the next applied Reveal_Action and says the action would reveal a Tile that cannot yet be proven safe, without saying whether any Tile is a mine or which of a Chord_Click's Target_Tiles is not Provably_Safe.
4. WHILE stage 1 of a Nudge is displayed, WHEN the learner performs another Intercepted_Action or selects the More Help control, THE Hint_Coach SHALL replace stage 1 with stage 2 for the same Hint_Target within 100 milliseconds of the input on the Reference_Device.
5. WHILE stage 2 of a Nudge is displayed, THE Hint_Coach SHALL show the More Help control as disabled and SHALL keep stage 2 for the same Hint_Target displayed, with nothing added, after each further Intercepted_Action.
6. WHEN the Hint_Coach starts a new Nudge, THE Hint_Coach SHALL choose as the Hint_Target the Tile that the Solver classifies as Provably_Safe in the current Board_State with the smallest Chebyshev distance (the larger of the absolute row difference and the absolute column difference) to the clicked Tile of the Intercepted_Action that started the Nudge, breaking ties first by Deduction_Level, preferring Single_Clue, then Pair_Clue, then Multi_Clue, then Mine_Count, and then by the first Tile in row-major order (top row first, left to right within a row).
7. THE Hint_Coach SHALL draw stage 1 of a Nudge as only an outline around the Hint_Region, and SHALL draw stage 2 as only that outline plus a highlight on each of the Hint_Target's Anchor_Numbers as the Pattern_Detector attributes them in the current Board_State and, when the Hint_Target's Deduction_Level is Mine_Count, a highlight on the mine counter.
8. FOR ALL Board_States and all sequences of Intercepted_Actions and More Help selections, displaying interception messages and Nudge stages SHALL leave the Board_State and the Move_Log unchanged, SHALL neither flag nor reveal any Tile, and SHALL add nothing to the Tiles or the mine counter except the Hint_Region outline, the highlights on the Anchor_Numbers, and the mine-counter highlight (guardrail property).
9. WHEN the learner performs a Flag_Toggle, or a Chord_Click that is a Wasted_Click, in an Active_Game, THE Practice_Mode SHALL apply the action, including a Flag_Toggle that places a Wrong_Flag, without displaying an interception message or starting a Nudge, and SHALL leave any displayed interception message and Nudge unchanged.
10. WHILE the current Board_State of an Active_Game is an Inexact_State, THE Practice_Mode SHALL apply each Reveal_Action whose Target_Tiles are all non-mine Tiles, including Target_Tiles that the Solver classifies as Undetermined, SHALL leave unapplied each Reveal_Action that has a mine among its Target_Tiles, and SHALL start no Nudge.
11. WHEN the learner reveals every non-mine Tile of a Practice_Mode board, THE Practice_Mode SHALL record that board as a completed Practice_Mode board for its Difficulty with the UTC time of completion, and SHALL display the number of Intercepted_Actions and the number of Nudges that reached stage 2 on that board.
12. WHILE the Solver's classification of the current Board_State is not ready, WHEN the learner performs a Player_Action in an Active_Game, THE Practice_Mode SHALL hold the action and apply or intercept held actions one at a time in input order, each once the Solver's classification of the Board_State it acts on is ready, counting the display time limits of this requirement and of Requirement 2.4 from that moment, and SHALL display a busy indicator whenever a held action has waited more than 100 milliseconds.
13. WHILE no Nudge is displayed, WHEN the learner performs an Intercepted_Action in a Board_State that is not an Inexact_State, THE Hint_Coach SHALL start a new Nudge and display its stage 1 together with an enabled More Help control within 100 milliseconds of the input on the Reference_Device.
14. FOR ALL No_Guess_Boards and all sequences of Player_Actions in Practice_Mode, the Practice_Mode SHALL apply no Reveal_Action that would reveal a mine, and no Practice_Mode game SHALL end in a loss (cannot-lose property).
