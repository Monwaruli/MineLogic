### Requirement 7: Pattern Detection

**User Story:** As a learner, I want MineLogic to recognize which taught pattern proves each safe tile, so that hints, replays, and speed measures refer to the patterns I studied.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN the Solver has classified the Board_State produced by an applied Reveal_Action in an Active_Game, or the initial Board_State of an Active_Game on a Board with an initial revealed set, THE Pattern_Detector SHALL give every Provably_Safe covered Tile in that Board_State exactly one Deduction_Level, exactly one Pattern_Type, and exactly one set of Anchor_Numbers. The Deduction_Level SHALL be the lowest level, in the order Single_Clue, Pair_Clue, Multi_Clue, Mine_Count, at which the Tile is provable from the Board_State's revealed Numbers and the Tile's Prior_Mines.
2. THE Pattern_Detector SHALL attribute to Satisfied_Number every Single_Clue Tile that became Provably_Safe after a Reveal_Action and borders a revealed Number whose Reduced_Value is zero. THE Pattern_Detector SHALL attribute every other Single_Clue Tile to Exposed_Mines, including every Tile that was already Provably_Safe in the initial Board_State of a Board with an initial revealed set.
3. THE Pattern_Detector SHALL attribute every Pair_Clue Tile that a Wall_Run proves to that Wall_Run's Pattern_Type (Wall_1_1, Wall_1_2_1, or Wall_1_2_2_1) and use the Wall_Run's Numbers as the Tile's Anchor_Numbers. It SHALL attribute every other Pair_Clue Tile to Subset, every Multi_Clue Tile to Clue_Chain, and every Mine_Count Tile to Mine_Count_Endgame.
4. WHEN a Reveal_Action that does not end the game is applied in an Active_Game, or an Active_Game on a Board with an initial revealed set starts, THE Pattern_Detector SHALL group into Pattern_Instances the covered Tiles that are Provably_Safe in the resulting Board_State and were not Provably_Safe before it, with one Pattern_Instance per distinct combination of Pattern_Type and Anchor_Numbers. Each new Pattern_Instance SHALL get the status open and an appearance time equal to the action's Move_Log time, or 0 for the initial Board_State.
5. WHEN the Target_Tiles of an applied Reveal_Action include a covered Tile of an open Pattern_Instance, including a Reveal_Action that ends the game, THE Pattern_Detector SHALL set that Pattern_Instance's status to resolved and record the action's Move_Log time as its resolution time.
6. IF a Reveal_Action reveals the last covered Tile of an open Pattern_Instance and none of that Pattern_Instance's Tiles was a Target_Tile of any Reveal_Action, so that every one of its Tiles was revealed by a Cascade, THEN THE Pattern_Detector SHALL set that Pattern_Instance's status to excluded.
7. IF, after a Reveal_Action that does not resolve an open Pattern_Instance, a still-covered Tile of that Pattern_Instance has a lower Deduction_Level than it had at the Pattern_Instance's appearance, THEN THE Pattern_Detector SHALL set that Pattern_Instance's status to superseded.
8. IF the Board_State immediately before or immediately after a Reveal_Action is an Inexact_State, THEN THE Pattern_Detector SHALL give every Pattern_Instance that appears at that Reveal_Action the status excluded instead of open.
9. WHEN an Active_Game ends with the outcome Won, Lost, Timed_Out, or Interrupted, THE Pattern_Detector SHALL set the status of every open Pattern_Instance to unresolved and leave the status of every other Pattern_Instance unchanged, so that each Pattern_Instance ends with exactly one of the statuses resolved, excluded, superseded, or unresolved.
10. WHEN the Pattern_Detector processes a Board's Canonical_Solve_Path, THE Pattern_Detector SHALL treat as a Reveal_Click both the Start_Tile reveal (for a full Board) and each step's reveal of the first Provably_Safe covered Tile in row-major order. It SHALL apply criteria 1 to 7 with the step number in place of the Move_Log time, and report for each Pattern_Type the number of Pattern_Instances that a step resolves.
11. FOR ALL authored attribution fixtures in the test suite, the Pattern_Detector SHALL attribute each fixture's labeled Tiles to the fixture's labeled Pattern_Type and Anchor_Numbers. The fixtures SHALL include at least one fixture for each of the eight Pattern_Types, plus a pair of fixtures that differ only in whether a satisfied Number's mines were Prior_Mines (labeled Satisfied_Number) or became Provably_Mine in the same Reveal_Action (labeled Exposed_Mines) (example-based check).
