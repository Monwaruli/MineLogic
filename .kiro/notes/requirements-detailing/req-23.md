### Requirement 23: Learning Phase and Group Assignment

**User Story:** As the researcher, I want participants split evenly at random between tutorial-plus-video and tutorial-only on Intermediate, so that I can measure what the video adds.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN the Learning_Phase begins after the Pre_Test (Requirement 22.1), THE Study_Module SHALL run the Skill_Check under the Skill_Check rules of Requirement 10, with no Skip control and with no result stored as the browser's Guest or Account progress, then the Beginner, Intermediate, and Expert learning blocks, and then the Post_Test, starting each of these when the one before it ends and starting the Intermediate learning block only after the Backend has returned the Participant's group. A learning block ends when all three of its parts are complete or the Participant selects its Continue control.
2. THE Study_Module SHALL compose each learning block, for every Participant whatever the group, of three parts in this order: the block Difficulty's Lessons in Requirement 11.1 order, then that Difficulty's Mastery_Checkpoint, then one Practice_Mode board of that Difficulty (Requirement 14.1). It SHALL open the Mastery_Checkpoint, the Practice_Mode board, and the step after the block only through a control shown once the part before it is complete, and SHALL judge completion only from the Participant's activity in the current Study_Session, storing no completion as the browser's Guest or Account progress. The Lessons part is complete when every Lesson of the Difficulty is completed (Requirement 11.9), the Mastery_Checkpoint part when a Checkpoint_Attempt for the Difficulty is passed (Requirement 13.4), and the Practice_Mode part when its board is completed (Requirement 14.11).
3. WHEN a Participant who has no group reaches the Intermediate learning block, either because the Beginner learning block has ended or because the Participant resumes the Study_Session at the Intermediate learning block, THE Backend SHALL assign the Participant to whichever of Group_A and Group_B has fewer assigned Participants in the same Study_Config, counting only Participants who have not withdrawn, and SHALL choose Group_A or Group_B with probability 1/2 each when the two counts are equal.
4. FOR ALL sequences of assignment requests and withdrawals in one Study_Config, including concurrent requests and repeated requests for the same Participant, each Participant SHALL have no group before reaching the Intermediate learning block and exactly one group from the first assignment on, which never changes and is returned for every repeated request, and each assignment SHALL leave the numbers of Participants in Group_A and in Group_B, counting only Participants who have not withdrawn, differing by at most one if they differed by at most one immediately before the assignment, and by one less than immediately before it otherwise (balance property).
5. WHEN a Participant who already has a group resumes a Study_Session, whether by reopening the Study_Link in the same browser (Requirement 21.9), by entering the Resume_Code in another browser (Requirement 21.10), or by reloading the page, THE Study_Module SHALL apply that same group under criteria 6 and 7 for the rest of the Study_Session.
6. WHILE the Tutorial_Player shows an Intermediate Lesson to a Group_A Participant, THE Study_Module SHALL show the Video_Panel with the Intermediate Difficulty's Video_Source, placed beside or below the Tutorial_Player as Requirement 12 criteria 2 and 3 define.
7. WHILE a Group_B Participant is in the Intermediate learning block, THE Study_Module SHALL hide the Video_Panel on every screen of the block, showing no video, placeholder, video control, or link to any video.
8. WHILE the Tutorial_Player shows a Beginner or Expert Lesson to a Participant, with or without a group, THE Study_Module SHALL show the Video_Panel for that Lesson's Difficulty, placed as Requirement 12 criteria 2 and 3 define, with the placeholder of Requirement 12 criterion 5 when that Difficulty has no Video_Source.
9. WHEN a learning block has run for its configured duration, THE Study_Module SHALL, within 1 second, show on every screen of the block, including screens shown after a resume, a Continue control that ends the block and opens the step after it (criterion 1), while the current part keeps accepting the Participant's actions until the Participant selects the control, and SHALL show no Continue control in the block before then. The configured duration is the Study_Config's duration for the block's Difficulty when it is a whole number of minutes from 1 to 30, and 8 minutes otherwise, measured on the Participant's computer clock from the first display of the block's first part, including time while the tab is hidden or the page is closed.
10. WHEN a Participant selects the Continue control of a learning block that has an incomplete part, THE Study_Logger SHALL record the block's Difficulty, the time of the selection, and each incomplete part, listing for an incomplete Lessons part each Lesson of that Difficulty not completed in the Study_Session.
11. IF the Backend has not returned a group for a Participant who has none within 10 seconds after the Participant reaches the Intermediate learning block, including when the Backend cannot be reached, THEN THE Study_Module SHALL show no part of the Intermediate learning block, display an error message stating that the study cannot continue until the connection is restored, with a retry control that requests the group again under the same 10-second limit, and keep the Participant's recorded progress unchanged.
12. WHILE a Skill_Check_Snippet, a Mastery_Checkpoint, a Practice_Mode board, or any other Learning_Phase screen without a Lesson is shown to a Participant, THE Study_Module SHALL hide the Video_Panel, including when a Mastery_Checkpoint shares a screen with the Tutorial_Player.
13. WHEN a Participant resumes a Study_Session during the Learning_Phase, THE Study_Module SHALL keep every Skill_Check answer and completion already recorded in the Study_Session and open the first incomplete item in the order of criteria 1 and 2: the first Skill_Check_Snippet without an answer, or else the current learning block's first incomplete part, opening the Lessons part at the first Lesson not completed in the Study_Session at the step that Requirement 11.10 selects, the Mastery_Checkpoint part with a new Checkpoint_Attempt, and the Practice_Mode part with a newly supplied Practice_Mode board.
