### Requirement 19: Leaderboards Gated Behind Practice

**User Story:** As a competitive player, I want leaderboards that open only after untimed practice, so that competition rewards accurate logic rather than reckless speed.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Leaderboard_Service SHALL keep one leaderboard per Difficulty, with no reset or time window, that holds at most one entry per Account: the Account's Leaderboard_Eligible result, among its won Timed_Games at that Difficulty that had no Assigned_Fixed_Board, that comes first in the order of criterion 3, so that no Daily_Sweep, Pre_Test, or Post_Test game enters a Difficulty leaderboard.
2. THE Leaderboard_Service SHALL keep one leaderboard per Sweep_Day that holds at most one entry per Account: the Account's First_Attempt of that Sweep_Day, held only while that First_Attempt is a Leaderboard_Eligible win, so that an Account whose First_Attempt of the Sweep_Day was lost or was played as a Guest has no entry for that Sweep_Day.
3. THE Leaderboard_Service SHALL order each leaderboard's entries by the elapsed milliseconds in each result's Game_Metrics, lowest first, then by the Backend time at which the Leaderboard_Service accepted the result, earliest first, then by Display_Name in Unicode code point order, and SHALL number the entries with consecutive ranks starting at 1, with no two entries sharing a rank.
4. IF an Account submits a winning result of a Timed_Game without an Assigned_Fixed_Board, and the Account's stored progress, including progress merged from Guest progress, holds no completed Practice_Mode board of the game's Difficulty whose completion time is earlier than the result's Game_Start_Time, THEN THE Leaderboard_Service SHALL reject the result, leave every leaderboard unchanged, and return the name of the failed check.
5. IF an Account submits a winning Daily_Sweep result, and either a Daily_Sweep game of the same Sweep_Day with an earlier Game_Start_Time is already stored for the Account or the Account's stored progress, including progress merged from Guest progress, holds no completed Practice_Mode board of any Difficulty whose completion time is earlier than the result's Game_Start_Time, THEN THE Leaderboard_Service SHALL reject the result, leave every leaderboard unchanged, and return the name of the failed check, checking the First_Attempt rule first.
6. IF a Timed_Game outside a Study_Session ends as a win and, judged from the player's recorded progress, its result would be rejected under criterion 4 or 5, THEN THE Timed_Game summary SHALL state that the result cannot enter the leaderboard and name each unmet condition: a completed Practice_Mode board needed at the game's Difficulty (at any Difficulty for a Daily_Sweep game), or the rule that only the First_Attempt of a Sweep_Day can enter that Sweep_Day's leaderboard.
7. IF a submitted result has an outcome other than Won, a Board whose width, height, or mine count differs from those of its Difficulty (Intermediate for a Daily_Sweep result), for a Daily_Sweep result a Board identifier other than that of its Sweep_Day's Daily_Sweep_Board or a receipt time more than 3,600 seconds after its Sweep_Day ended, an elapsed time below its 3BV divided by 15 seconds compared without rounding (a 3BV of 40 requires at least 2,667 milliseconds), an elapsed time above 3,600,000 milliseconds, or a Game_Identifier of an already accepted result that came from another Account or differs in any submitted value, THEN THE Leaderboard_Service SHALL reject the result, leave every leaderboard unchanged, and return the name of the first failed check in the order listed, taking the Board from the result's Board_Format text and the elapsed milliseconds and 3BV from its Game_Metrics, and applying these checks after criterion 8 and before criteria 4 and 5.
8. IF the Leaderboard_Service receives a result from an Account while it has received 30 or more of that Account's results in the preceding 3,600 seconds, counting each Game_Identifier once and not counting results rejected under this criterion, THEN THE Leaderboard_Service SHALL reject the result without applying any other check, leave every leaderboard unchanged, and return the name of the failed check.
9. THE Leaderboard_Service SHALL let Guests and Accounts read every leaderboard, including those of past Sweep_Days, and SHALL return for each entry only its rank, Display_Name, elapsed milliseconds, Logical_Move_Rate (as not available when the result's Game_Metrics report it so), and Chording_Efficiency, with no other value such as an Account identifier, email address, Game_Identifier, or Move_Log.
10. WHEN the Hub is displayed, THE Hub SHALL read the three Difficulty leaderboards and the current Sweep_Day's leaderboard from the Leaderboard_Service and show each one's first 50 entries in rank order (all entries when there are fewer than 50, or a message that there are no entries yet when there are none), with each entry's rank, Display_Name, elapsed time in seconds to three decimal places, Logical_Move_Rate, and Chording_Efficiency, and, for a signed-in player, the player's own rank on each of those leaderboards or a statement that the player has no entry on it.
11. WHEN a Guest wins a Timed_Game outside a Study_Session, THE Timed_Game summary SHALL display a sign-in control and a statement that only wins made while signed in to an Account can enter leaderboards.
12. WHEN a Timed_Game outside a Study_Session ends as a win while the player is signed in to an Account, THE MineLogic client SHALL submit the result, with its Game_Identifier, its Game_Start_Time, and the items that Requirement 9 requires for every stored game, to the Leaderboard_Service through the Sync_Queue after every write that the Sync_Queue held when the game ended, and SHALL never submit a result of a game that ended while the player was a Guest, including after that Guest progress is merged into an Account.
13. FOR ALL results that the Leaderboard_Service has accepted, delivering the same result again any number of times, with the same Game_Identifier and the same submitted values, SHALL report the result as accepted, including while criterion 8 would reject a new result, and SHALL leave every leaderboard and the criterion 8 count unchanged (idempotence property).
14. IF the Hub cannot read a leaderboard from the Leaderboard_Service within 10 seconds of being displayed, THEN THE Hub SHALL display, in place of that leaderboard's entries, a message that the leaderboard is unavailable, and SHALL still display every other part of the Hub.
