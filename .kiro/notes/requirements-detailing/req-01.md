### Requirement 1: Minesweeper Game Rules

**User Story:** As a learner, I want the board to follow the classic Minesweeper rules, so that what I learn transfers to any Minesweeper game.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN a full Board is loaded, THE Board_View SHALL display every Tile as covered and unflagged, with a start marker on the Start_Tile only that uses a shape or text label different from the flag marker and stays until the Start_Tile is revealed.
2. WHILE the Start_Tile of a full Board is covered, IF a Reveal_Click targets a Tile other than the Start_Tile or a Flag_Toggle targets any Tile, THEN THE Board_Engine SHALL leave the Board_State and the Move_Log unchanged and display a prompt to click the marked Start_Tile until the Start_Tile is revealed.
3. WHEN a Reveal_Click targets the covered Start_Tile of a full Board, THE Board_Engine SHALL start the game at an elapsed time of 0 milliseconds and reveal the Start_Tile's Opening.
4. WHILE the Board_Engine holds an Active_Game, WHEN a Reveal_Click targets a covered, unflagged, non-mine Tile, THE Board_Engine SHALL reveal that Tile with its Number, shown as a digit from 1 to 8, or as a blank revealed Tile when the Number is zero.
5. WHEN a zero-Number Tile is revealed, THE Board_Engine SHALL, within the same Player_Action, reveal every covered, unflagged Neighbor of that Tile and of every zero-Number Tile revealed by this Cascade, leaving every flagged Tile covered and flagged.
6. WHILE the Board_Engine holds an Active_Game, WHEN a Flag_Toggle targets a covered Tile, THE Board_Engine SHALL change the Tile from unflagged to flagged or from flagged to unflagged, with no limit on the number of flagged Tiles.
7. WHILE the Board_Engine holds an Active_Game, WHEN a Chord_Click targets a revealed Number whose count of flagged Neighbors equals the Number and that has at least one covered, unflagged Neighbor, THE Board_Engine SHALL reveal every covered, unflagged Neighbor of that Number within that one Chord_Click, including any such Neighbor that is a mine.
8. WHILE the Board_Engine holds an Active_Game, IF a Chord_Click targets a revealed Number, including a revealed zero-Number Tile, and either its count of flagged Neighbors differs from the Number or it has no covered, unflagged Neighbor, THEN THE Board_Engine SHALL leave the Board_State unchanged and record the Chord_Click in the Move_Log as a Wasted_Click.
9. IF a Reveal_Action reveals one or more mines, THEN THE Board_Engine SHALL end the game as a loss, even when that action also leaves every non-mine Tile revealed, and SHALL show every mine revealed by that action with an exploded-mine marker, reveal every other unflagged mine, keep every flag that is on a mine, and show every flag on a non-mine Tile with an incorrect-flag marker.
10. WHEN a Reveal_Action leaves every non-mine Tile revealed and reveals no mine, THE Board_Engine SHALL end the game as a win and place a flag on every unflagged mine without adding any entry to the Move_Log other than the End_Entry.
11. THE Board_View SHALL display a mine counter equal to the Board's mine count minus the number of flagged Tiles, showing a negative value with a minus sign when more Tiles are flagged than the Board has mines.
12. WHEN the Board_Engine applies a Player_Action, including a Wasted_Click, THE Board_Engine SHALL append exactly one entry to the Move_Log with the action's type, its Tile, whether it is a Wasted_Click, and its time in whole milliseconds since the game started, with the Start_Tile's Reveal_Click at time 0 and no entry's time lower than the time of the entry before it.
13. WHILE the Board_Engine holds a game that has ended, THE Board_Engine SHALL apply no Player_Action and SHALL leave the Board_State, the outcome, and the Move_Log unchanged.
14. IF the Board_Engine is given a Player_Action of type Reveal_Click for a revealed or flagged Tile, of type Flag_Toggle for a revealed Tile, of type Chord_Click for a covered Tile, or of any type for a Tile outside the Board's width and height, THEN THE Board_Engine SHALL leave the Board_State and the Move_Log unchanged and return an error that identifies the action and the rule it breaks.
15. FOR ALL Boards accepted by the Board_Parser and FOR ALL sequences of Player_Actions given to the Board_Engine, every Tile revealed before an action SHALL still be revealed after it, no action SHALL reveal a flagged Tile, and every Move_Log entry SHALL be marked as a Wasted_Click if and only if its action left the Board_State unchanged (reveal-monotonicity, flag-protection, and Wasted_Click properties).
