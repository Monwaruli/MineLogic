### Requirement 18: Daily Sweep and Daily Streak

**User Story:** As a returning player, I want one shared daily board that mixes the patterns I have learned, so that a short daily habit keeps my streak alive and lets me compare times on the same board.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. FOR ALL pairs of Daily_Sweep game requests made while the Current_Sweep_Day is the same Sweep_Day, from any players, devices, browsers, device clock settings, and time zone settings, the supplied Boards SHALL be equal under Board_Equality, including when the Board assigned to that Sweep_Day or the contents of the Sweep_Reserve change after the first of the two requests (same-board property).
2. THE Content_Validator SHALL verify that every Board assigned to a Sweep_Day and every Board in the Sweep_Reserve is a Well_Formed_Board with a Start_Tile, width 16, height 16, and 40 mines; is a No_Guess_Board with no Inexact_State on its Canonical_Solve_Path; and, among the Pattern_Instances that Requirement 7 criterion 10 reports as resolved on that Canonical_Solve_Path, has at least one of a Beginner-tier Pattern_Type (Exposed_Mines or Satisfied_Number), at least one of an Intermediate-tier Pattern_Type (Wall_1_1, Wall_1_2_1, or Wall_1_2_2_1), and at least one of an Expert-tier Pattern_Type (Subset, Clue_Chain, or Mine_Count_Endgame). THE Content_Validator SHALL also verify that the Board's mine positions, as placed, rotated, or reflected, match those of no Puzzle_Bank Board, no Evaluation_Board, and no Board in any other Sweep_Day assignment or Sweep_Reserve place.
3. IF no Board is assigned to a Sweep_Day when that Sweep_Day's Daily_Sweep_Board is first supplied, THEN THE Daily_Sweep SHALL use as that Sweep_Day's Daily_Sweep_Board the Sweep_Reserve Board chosen by a rule that depends only on the Sweep_Day's UTC date and the Boards in the Sweep_Reserve. While the Sweep_Reserve holds N Boards, with N at least 2, and does not change, the rule SHALL choose N different Boards for any N consecutive Sweep_Days that have no assigned Board.
4. WHEN a player requests a Daily_Sweep game, THE Daily_Sweep SHALL obtain the Current_Sweep_Day's Daily_Sweep_Board through the Board_Provider as the game's Assigned_Fixed_Board and play it as a Timed_Game under Requirements 15 and 16, whatever the outcomes of the player's earlier Sweep_Attempts on that Sweep_Day, and SHALL include the Sweep_Attempt's Sweep_Day and attempt number in the game result stored under Requirement 15 criterion 8.
5. WHEN a Sweep_Attempt ends with the outcome Won, THE Streak_Tracker SHALL add the Sweep_Attempt's Sweep_Day to the player's set of completed Sweep_Days, including when the win comes after that Sweep_Day has ended, whatever the attempt number or Leaderboard_Eligible status, and for Guests and Accounts alike.
6. IF a Sweep_Attempt ends with the outcome Lost, THEN THE Daily_Sweep SHALL offer in the Timed_Game summary a control that makes a new Daily_Sweep game request under criterion 4, with no limit on the number of Sweep_Attempts per Sweep_Day, and during the same Sweep_Day that request SHALL start a new game on the same Daily_Sweep_Board with every Tile covered.
7. THE Streak_Tracker SHALL compute the Daily_Streak for a current Sweep_Day D from only D and the player's set of completed Sweep_Days, including Sweep_Days whose Backend write is still waiting in the Sync_Queue and ignoring Sweep_Days later than D. If D is in the set, the Daily_Streak SHALL be the length of the run of consecutive Sweep_Days in the set that ends at D. If D is not in the set and the Sweep_Day before D is, it SHALL be the length of the run that ends at the Sweep_Day before D. If neither is in the set, it SHALL be 0.
8. FOR ALL sets of completed Sweep_Days and FOR ALL current Sweep_Days D, adding D to a set that contains the Sweep_Day before D but not D SHALL increase the Daily_Streak by exactly one, adding D to a set that contains neither D nor the Sweep_Day before D SHALL change the Daily_Streak from zero to one, and adding any Sweep_Day to any set SHALL never decrease the Daily_Streak (streak properties).
9. THE Streak_Tracker SHALL compute the longest Daily_Streak as the length of the longest run of consecutive Sweep_Days in the player's set of completed Sweep_Days, or 0 when the set is empty. FOR ALL sets and current Sweep_Days, the longest Daily_Streak SHALL be at least the Daily_Streak, and adding a Sweep_Day SHALL never decrease it (longest-streak property).
10. WHILE the Hub is displayed, THE Hub SHALL display the time remaining until the Current_Sweep_Day ends, as hours, minutes, and seconds rounded down to whole seconds, updated at least once per second.
11. IF the Daily_Sweep_Board for a Daily_Sweep game request cannot be supplied, because the Backend cannot be reached, the Board_Parser rejects the Board's text, the Board lacks a Start_Tile or does not have width 16, height 16, and 40 mines, or no Board is assigned to the Current_Sweep_Day and the Sweep_Reserve holds no Board, THEN THE Daily_Sweep SHALL start no game, substitute no Board from the Board_Generator or the Puzzle_Bank, show the error message and retry control of Requirement 6 criterion 8, and leave the player's Sweep_Attempts and completed Sweep_Days unchanged.
12. WHEN the Start_Tile of a Daily_Sweep game is revealed, THE Daily_Sweep SHALL record the new Sweep_Attempt with its Sweep_Day and attempt number in the player's stored progress (Requirement 20) before the Start_Tile's Opening is displayed, and SHALL keep that record when the Sweep_Attempt is abandoned, so that only the player's first Sweep_Attempt of a Sweep_Day has attempt number 1 and counts as the First_Attempt in Requirement 19 criteria 2 and 5.
13. WHILE the Hub is displayed, WHEN a new Sweep_Day begins, THE Hub SHALL, within 1 second, display the Daily_Streak and the longest Daily_Streak computed for the new Current_Sweep_Day and show the countdown of criterion 10 for that Sweep_Day.
