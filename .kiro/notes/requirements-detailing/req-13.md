### Requirement 13: Pattern Mastery Checkpoints

**User Story:** As a learner, I want an unassisted check at the end of each tutorial, so that a full progress bar means I can apply the patterns on my own.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN the learner starts a Mastery_Checkpoint for a Difficulty, THE Mastery_Checkpoint SHALL begin a new Checkpoint_Attempt with the consecutive-success count at zero and present one of that Difficulty's Checkpoint_Variations.
2. WHEN the learner clears a Checkpoint_Variation by revealing every non-mine Tile without a Slip, THE Mastery_Checkpoint SHALL stop accepting Player_Actions on that Checkpoint_Variation, keep it displayed, increase the consecutive-success count by one, and display a Next control if the count is then below three.
3. IF the learner makes a Slip, THEN THE Mastery_Checkpoint SHALL leave the action unapplied, reset the consecutive-success count to zero, stop accepting Player_Actions on that Checkpoint_Variation while keeping it displayed, display the Next control, and mark the Tile of the Wrong_Flag, or each Target_Tile of the Guess that was not Provably_Safe, with an error indicator that has an icon or text label in addition to color, within 100 milliseconds on the Reference_Device after the later of the input and the moment the Solver's classification of the Board_State before the action is ready.
4. WHEN the consecutive-success count reaches three, THE Mastery_Checkpoint SHALL end the Checkpoint_Attempt without presenting another Checkpoint_Variation, display a message that the Difficulty's Mastery_Checkpoint is passed, and record the Mastery_Checkpoint for that Difficulty as passed in the Progress_Service, where it stays passed whatever the results of later Checkpoint_Attempts for that Difficulty.
5. WHILE a Checkpoint_Attempt is in progress, THE Mastery_Checkpoint SHALL display the consecutive-success count as three markers, with as many markers drawn filled as the current count and the rest drawn as empty outlines, and SHALL update the markers within 100 milliseconds on the Reference_Device after the count changes.
6. WHEN the learner selects the Next control, THE Mastery_Checkpoint SHALL present one of the Difficulty's Checkpoint_Variations that differs from each of the at most two Checkpoint_Variations presented immediately before it in the same Checkpoint_Attempt.
7. THE Content_Validator SHALL verify that each Difficulty has at least six Checkpoint_Variations, that no two Checkpoint_Variations of a Difficulty have both the same mine positions and the same initial revealed set, and that every Checkpoint_Variation is a Mini_Board whose Canonical_Solve_Path reveals every non-mine Tile with no Inexact_State and whose initial state has at least one Provably_Safe Tile, with every such Tile attributed to a Pattern_Type of the Checkpoint_Variation's Difficulty's tier.
8. WHILE a Checkpoint_Variation accepts Player_Actions, THE Mastery_Checkpoint SHALL display it as the only Board on screen, apply each Player_Action on it that is not a Slip according to Requirement 1, including flag removals and Wasted_Clicks, and display no Overlays, no Nudges, no Video_Panel, no text that names a Pattern_Type or says why a Tile is safe or a mine, and no marker on a covered Tile other than the learner's own flags.
9. IF the learner makes a Player_Action on a Checkpoint_Variation before the Solver's classification of its current Board_State is ready, THEN THE Mastery_Checkpoint SHALL hold the action, apply or intercept held actions one at a time in the order they were made as each classification becomes ready, discard any held action that remains when the Checkpoint_Variation stops accepting Player_Actions, and display a busy indicator from 100 milliseconds after the input until no action is held.
10. FOR ALL Checkpoint_Attempts and FOR ALL sequences of Player_Actions in them, no mine SHALL be revealed, the consecutive-success count SHALL equal the number of Checkpoint_Variations cleared since the later of the Checkpoint_Attempt's start and its most recent Slip, and the Checkpoint_Attempt SHALL end as passed exactly when three consecutively presented Checkpoint_Variations are cleared (checkpoint model property).
