### Requirement 22: Standardized Pre-Test and Post-Test

**User Story:** As the researcher, I want every participant to play the same fixed boards before and after the learning phase, so that before-and-after measures are comparable across participants.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN consent is recorded, THE Study_Module SHALL, after the Resume_Code display of Requirement 21, run the Study_Session steps in the order Background_Questions, Pre_Test, Learning_Phase, Post_Test, Survey_Form, completion screen, starting each step only after the step before it is complete and never reopening a complete step or an ended Test_Game. The Background_Questions SHALL use the choices and the Continue rule of Requirement 10 criterion 2, with no Skip control. The Pre_Test and the Post_Test SHALL each be complete when every one of their Test_Games has ended, whatever the outcome.
2. WHILE a Study_Session is in progress in the current browser, from the recorded consent until the completion screen is shown or a withdrawal is confirmed, THE Study_Module SHALL display on every Study_Session screen no control or link that opens the Hub, any other MineLogic mode, or any Study_Session step or Test_Game other than the current one, so that the only navigation controls are the current step's own controls and the Withdraw control of Requirement 21.
3. WHEN the Pre_Test begins, THE Study_Module SHALL run exactly two Test_Games, first on the Pre_Beginner and then on the Pre_Intermediate Evaluation_Board of the Study_Config under which the Participant consented, each supplied unchanged by the Board_Provider as the game's Assigned_Fixed_Board.
4. WHEN the Post_Test begins, THE Study_Module SHALL run exactly three Test_Games, on the Post_Beginner, then the Post_Intermediate, then the Post_Expert Evaluation_Board of the Study_Config under which the Participant consented, each supplied unchanged by the Board_Provider as the game's Assigned_Fixed_Board.
5. THE Content_Validator SHALL verify, for Post_Beginner paired with Pre_Beginner and for Post_Intermediate paired with Pre_Intermediate, that the two Boards have equal width, height, mine count, and 3BV; that the Post_Test Board's mine Tiles and Start_Tile are the Pre_Test Board's mine Tiles and Start_Tile rotated by 90, 180, or 270 degrees, or reflected across the horizontal axis, the vertical axis, or either diagonal; and that the two Boards' sets of mine Tiles, as placed, are not the same set.
6. THE Content_Validator SHALL verify that each Evaluation_Board is a Well_Formed_Board with a Start_Tile and with its Difficulty's width, height, and mine count (Beginner for Pre_Beginner and Post_Beginner, Intermediate for Pre_Intermediate and Post_Intermediate, Expert for Post_Expert); is a No_Guess_Board with no Inexact_State on its Canonical_Solve_Path; and, among the Pattern_Instances that Requirement 7 criterion 10 reports as resolved on that Canonical_Solve_Path, has at least one of a Beginner-tier Pattern_Type, plus at least one of an Intermediate-tier Pattern_Type for an Intermediate or Expert Board, plus at least one of an Expert-tier Pattern_Type for an Expert Board. THE Content_Validator SHALL also verify that the five Evaluation_Boards have five different identifiers, and that no Evaluation_Board's mine positions, as placed, rotated, or reflected, match those of a Puzzle_Bank Board, a Board assigned to a Sweep_Day, or a Sweep_Reserve Board.
7. WHEN the Pre_Test begins, THE Study_Module SHALL display, before the Pre_Beginner Test_Game's Board is displayed, a controls reminder that describes the Reveal_Click as a primary click on a covered Tile and the Flag_Toggle as a secondary click on a covered Tile, describes no other input, names neither the Chord_Click nor any Pattern_Type, and has a control that opens the Pre_Beginner Test_Game.
8. WHILE a Test_Game's Board is displayed and the game has not ended, THE Study_Module SHALL run the game as a Timed_Game under Requirement 15, SHALL accept every Chord_Click input of Requirement 2, as in every other Timed_Game, and SHALL display no control that restarts, retries, pauses, or skips the game.
9. WHEN the elapsed time of a Test_Game that is an Active_Game reaches the game's Time_Cap, counting every period in which the browser tab was hidden, THE Study_Module SHALL end the game with the outcome Timed_Out, write an End_Entry whose time is the Time_Cap in milliseconds, and apply and log no Player_Action made at or after the Time_Cap, so that the Game_Metrics come only from the Player_Actions made before the Time_Cap.
10. WHERE the Study_Config under which the Participant consented sets no time cap for a Difficulty, or sets one that is not a whole number of seconds from 60 to 3,600, THE Study_Module SHALL give that Difficulty's Test_Games a Time_Cap of 240 seconds for Beginner, 480 seconds for Intermediate, or 720 seconds for Expert.
11. WHEN a Test_Game ends with the outcome Won, Lost, or Timed_Out, THE Study_Module SHALL replace the Board, within 500 milliseconds on the Reference_Device (counted from when the tab next becomes visible if it is hidden when the game ends), with an end screen that shows only the outcome, the elapsed time in seconds with three decimal places, and a Continue control that opens the next Test_Game or, after the last Test_Game of the Pre_Test or Post_Test, the next Study_Session step, and that shows no Game_Metric, mine position, replay control, or retry control, whether or not the Metrics_Engine returns Game_Metrics for the game.
12. IF the Participant reloads or closes the page, or leaves the Test_Game's screen in any other way (including with the browser's back or forward control or by opening another URL), while a Test_Game's Board is displayed and the game has not ended, THEN THE Study_Module SHALL, no later than the next opening of the Study_Link in that browser, end the game with the outcome Interrupted, write an End_Entry whose time is that of the last Player_Action logged before the interruption (or 0 when none was logged), keep in the Move_Log every Player_Action that the Board_Engine applied before the interruption, resume the Study_Session at the next Test_Game or, after the last Test_Game of the Pre_Test or Post_Test, at the next Study_Session step, and show a message that the interrupted game was recorded and cannot be played again.
13. IF the Board_Provider cannot supply a Test_Game's Evaluation_Board, because the Backend cannot be reached, the Board_Parser rejects the Board's text, the Board lacks a Start_Tile, or the Board does not have its Difficulty's width, height, and mine count, THEN THE Study_Module SHALL start no game, substitute no Board from the Board_Generator or the Puzzle_Bank, show the error message and retry control of Requirement 6 criterion 8, record no outcome and no Study_Record for that Test_Game, and keep the Study_Session at that Test_Game until its Board is supplied.
14. FOR ALL Participants who consented under the same Study_Config, on any browser or device, and including Study_Sessions resumed by reopening the Study_Link or by entering a Resume_Code, the Board supplied for each Evaluation_Board's Test_Game SHALL be equal under Board_Equality to the Board supplied for that Evaluation_Board to every other such Participant, and every Test_Game of one Difficulty SHALL have the same Time_Cap, even when the Study_Config's Evaluation_Boards or time caps are edited after the first of those Participants' consent was recorded (same-board property).
15. WHEN a Test_Game ends with any outcome, THE Study_Module SHALL compute its Game_Metrics in one Metrics_Engine run under Requirement 9 criterion 1, with the same Metrics_Engine and Metrics_Version that compute every other Timed_Game's Game_Metrics in the same client build, and SHALL pass to the Study_Logger through the Sync_Queue, as the game's only stored result, the Participant_ID, the phase, the Difficulty, the Time_Cap in milliseconds, the items that Requirement 9 requires for every stored game, and that run's Game_Metrics unchanged (with Chording_Efficiency not available for every outcome other than Won), or the error's name in place of the Game_Metrics when the run returns an error. It SHALL add nothing for the game to the browser's Guest or Account progress and SHALL submit nothing to the Leaderboard_Service.
16. FOR ALL Test_Games that end with the outcome Won or Lost, every Game_Metric stored in the game's Study_Record, including not-available values and the partial mark, SHALL equal the value that the Timed_Game summary of Requirement 15 criterion 6 displays for a Timed_Game outside a Study_Session with the same Board, Move_Log, and Metrics_Version, and FOR ALL Test_Games with stored Game_Metrics, whatever the outcome, recomputing the Game_Metrics from the Study_Record's Board_Format text and Move_Log text with its Metrics_Version SHALL reproduce them exactly (same-measure property).
