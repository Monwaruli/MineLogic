### Requirement 11: Interactive Learn-Then-Do Tutorials

**User Story:** As a learner, I want short interactive lessons that show me why tiles are safe and then make me apply the trick myself, so that I learn each pattern by doing it.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Tutorial_Player SHALL provide, for each Difficulty, an ordered list of Lessons that splits that Difficulty's Pattern_Types, in the order given here, into consecutive groups of one or two, with exactly one Lesson teaching each group: Exposed_Mines (corner counting) then Satisfied_Number at Beginner; Wall_1_1, then Wall_1_2_1, then Wall_1_2_2_1 at Intermediate; and Subset, then Clue_Chain, then Mine_Count_Endgame at Expert.
2. WHEN a Learn_Step is shown, THE Tutorial_Player SHALL draw Overlays on the interacting Numbers (the Numbers that the Learn_Step's explanation uses) and on every Tile those Numbers prove safe or a mine, and SHALL display text that names the Pattern_Type being taught and states, for each marked Tile, whether it is safe or a mine and which marked Numbers prove it.
3. THE Tutorial_Player SHALL present each Lesson as one to four Learn_Steps followed by two or three Do_Steps, where each Do_Step has its own 5x5 Mini_Board, no two Do_Steps of the Lesson have both the same mine positions and the same initial revealed set, each Do_Step has one Practiced_Pattern_Type taken from the Lesson's taught Pattern_Types, and each Pattern_Type the Lesson teaches is the Practiced_Pattern_Type of at least one of its Do_Steps.
4. WHEN the learner performs, during a Do_Step, a Logical_Move, a Flag_Toggle that places a flag on a Provably_Mine Tile, or a Flag_Toggle that removes a flag, THE Tutorial_Player SHALL apply the action to the Mini_Board under the rules of Requirement 1, including any Cascade.
5. IF the learner performs, during a Do_Step, a Reveal_Action with a Target_Tile that is not Provably_Safe or a Flag_Toggle that would place a flag on a Tile that is not Provably_Mine, THEN THE Tutorial_Player SHALL leave the Mini_Board unchanged, mark the clicked Tile with an error indicator within 100 milliseconds on the Reference_Device, display an explanation that the Tile cannot yet be proven safe (for a Reveal_Action) or cannot yet be proven a mine (for a Flag_Toggle), and keep the indicator and explanation displayed until the learner's next Player_Action.
6. IF a Do_Step has a Chord_Objective and an applied Reveal_Click reveals one of its Objective_Tiles, either as the clicked Tile or through the Cascade the click starts, THEN THE Tutorial_Player SHALL reset the Mini_Board to its initial state, removing every reveal and flag made during the Do_Step, and display a message asking the learner to reveal the Objective_Tiles with a Chord_Click.
7. WHILE a Do_Step's Objective is unmet, THE Tutorial_Player SHALL keep the control for the next step disabled.
8. WHEN the learner meets a Do_Step's Objective, meaning every Objective_Tile is revealed and, for a Chord_Objective, every Objective_Tile was revealed by a Chord_Click, THE Tutorial_Player SHALL display a completion indicator for the Do_Step and enable the next-step control within 100 milliseconds on the Reference_Device, where the next step is the Lesson's next Do_Step, or, after the Lesson's last Do_Step, the Difficulty's next Lesson, or, after the Difficulty's last Lesson, the Difficulty's Mastery_Checkpoint.
9. WHEN the learner meets a Do_Step's Objective, THE Progress_Service SHALL record the Do_Step as complete as a milestone, and SHALL also record the Lesson as complete when that Do_Step is the Lesson's last Do_Step.
10. WHEN the learner opens a Lesson, THE Tutorial_Player SHALL show the first Do_Step not recorded as complete, with that Do_Step's Mini_Board in its initial state, if the Lesson is not recorded as complete and at least one of its Do_Steps is recorded as complete, and SHALL show the Lesson's first Learn_Step in every other case.
11. THE Content_Validator SHALL verify that every Tile a Learn_Step marks safe is Provably_Safe and every Tile it marks as a mine is Provably_Mine in that Learn_Step's Board_State, that every Do_Step's Objective_Tiles are covered, non-mine Tiles in its Mini_Board's initial state, and that every Do_Step's Objective can be met from that initial state using only the actions that criterion 4 applies.
12. IF the learner performs, during a Do_Step, a Chord_Click on a Number whose count of flagged Neighbors differs from the Number, THEN THE Tutorial_Player SHALL leave the Mini_Board unchanged, mark the Number with an error indicator within 100 milliseconds on the Reference_Device, and display an explanation that a Chord_Click reveals Tiles only when the Number's count of flagged Neighbors equals the Number.
13. FOR ALL Do_Steps and FOR ALL sequences of actions that criterion 4 applies, starting from the Do_Step's initial state and ending when its Objective is met, the Content_Validator SHALL verify that at least one Objective_Tile is attributed by the Pattern_Detector to the Do_Step's Practiced_Pattern_Type in the Board_State immediately before the action that reveals it, and, for a Do_Step whose Practiced_Pattern_Type is Satisfied_Number, that this Objective_Tile is not Provably_Safe in the initial state and becomes Provably_Safe only after an earlier action in the sequence (attribution property).
14. THE Tutorial_Player SHALL include in the Lesson that teaches Satisfied_Number a Learn_Step whose text names the three Chord_Click inputs (primary click, middle click, and simultaneous primary and secondary press on a revealed Number), and at least one Do_Step that has a Chord_Objective and has Satisfied_Number as its Practiced_Pattern_Type.
15. IF the learner makes a Player_Action during a Do_Step before the Solver's classification of the Mini_Board's current Board_State is ready, THEN THE Tutorial_Player SHALL hold the action, apply or reject held actions one at a time in input order as each classification becomes ready, and display a busy indicator whenever a held action has waited more than 100 milliseconds.
