### Requirement 24: In-App Survey and Retention Window

**User Story:** As the researcher, I want an in-app survey and a record of daily play in the five days after the session, so that I can measure perceived clarity, pacing, and motivation, and streak retention.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN the Study_Module moves to the Survey_Form step, either after the Post_Expert game ends with any outcome (Won, Lost, Timed_Out, or Interrupted) or on resuming a Study_Session whose first incomplete step is the Survey_Form, THE Survey_Form SHALL present the seven 5-point rating items, then the three open-ended prompts, then the one demographics item, keeping the Study_Config's item order within each group, with the demographics item allowing at most one of its answer options to be selected, and with every item unanswered, so that answers entered before a reload or a closed page are not kept.
2. WHEN the Study_Module moves to the Survey_Form step, THE Survey_Form SHALL read the survey items of the Participant's Study_Config from the Backend and show each item's text and the demographics item's answer options exactly as read, so that a change to those survey items applies to every Survey_Form presented after the change.
3. THE Survey_Form SHALL show each rating item's five points from left to right as 1, 2, 3, 4, and 5, label every point with its number, also label point 1 "strongly disagree" and point 5 "strongly agree", and let the Participant select at most one point per rating item, with a new selection replacing the earlier one.
4. WHEN the Participant selects the submit control, THE Survey_Form SHALL accept the submission on that one selection, with no confirmation step and no required item, whether none, some, or all 11 items are Unanswered_Items.
5. THE Survey_Form SHALL accept in each open-ended prompt a multi-line answer of 0 to 2,000 characters, where each Unicode code point, including each line break, counts as one character, SHALL insert only the leading characters of newly typed or pasted text that keep the answer within 2,000 characters, and SHALL show beside each open-ended prompt the number of characters remaining (2,000 minus the answer's length), updated within 100 milliseconds of each change on the Reference_Device.
6. WHEN the Survey_Form accepts a submission, THE Study_Logger SHALL store one Survey_Response with the Participant_ID, the Survey_Submission_Day, and, for each of the 11 items, the item's position from 1 to 11 in presentation order, the item text shown, and the answer: the selected point as a whole number from 1 to 5 for a rating item, the selected option's text for the demographics item, the text exactly as entered for an open-ended prompt, or a blank for an Unanswered_Item.
7. WHEN the Survey_Form accepts a submission, or when a Participant whose submission the Survey_Form has accepted reopens the Study_Link or enters the Participant's Resume_Code, THE Study_Module SHALL show the completion screen with the Participant's Resume_Code, the UTC dates of the first and last Sweep_Days of the Participant's Retention_Window, a statement that playing the Daily_Sweep on those Sweep_Days is optional, and a statement that Daily_Sweep games count for the study only in a browser that holds the Participant's Study_Identity, such as this browser or one in which the Resume_Code has been entered. After an accepted submission, THE Study_Module SHALL show this screen within 1 second on the Reference_Device without waiting for the Backend to confirm the Survey_Response write.
8. WHILE a browser holds the Study_Identity of a Participant who has not withdrawn, WHEN a Sweep_Attempt in that browser ends with the outcome Won or Lost and its Sweep_Day is one of the five Sweep_Days of that Participant's Retention_Window, THE Study_Logger SHALL store one Study_Record with the Participant_ID, the phase Retention_Window, the Sweep_Day, the attempt number, the outcome, the elapsed milliseconds, the Board identifier, the Board's canonical Board_Format text, the Metrics_Version, the Game_Metrics, and the serialized Move_Log including the End_Entry, and with no Account identifier or Display_Name, including when the Sweep_Attempt ends after its Sweep_Day has ended.
9. WHEN a Daily_Sweep game ends in a browser that holds a Participant's Study_Identity, THE MineLogic client SHALL store the result, update the completed Sweep_Days, and submit the result to the Leaderboard_Service under the same Guest and Account rules as for any other player (Requirements 15, 18, 19, and 20), in addition to any Study_Record of criterion 8, so that the result enters a leaderboard only if the Participant was signed in to an Account when the game ended, and then only under that Account's Display_Name, never under an alias, with no Participant_ID or Resume_Code sent to the Leaderboard_Service.
10. IF, when the Study_Module moves to the Survey_Form step, the survey items of the Participant's Study_Config cannot be read from the Backend within 10 seconds, or they are not exactly seven rating items, three open-ended prompts, and one demographics item, each with text of 1 to 500 characters, with 2 to 10 distinct demographics answer options of 1 to 100 characters each, THEN THE Study_Module SHALL show in place of the Survey_Form an error message stating that the survey could not be loaded, with a retry control that reads the survey items again, store no Survey_Response, and keep the Survey_Form as the Participant's first incomplete step.
11. FOR ALL Survey_Form submissions, including open-ended answers of 0 to 2,000 characters that contain line breaks, commas, quotation marks, and non-ASCII characters, the Survey_Response read back from the Backend SHALL contain each answer exactly as submitted and a blank for each Unanswered_Item (storage round-trip property), and FOR ALL sequences of submissions for one Participant_ID, including repeated selections of the submit control, repeated Sync_Queue deliveries, and submissions from two browsers, the Backend SHALL hold exactly one Survey_Response for that Participant_ID, equal to the first one it accepted (single-response property).
12. FOR ALL Participants who have not withdrawn and FOR ALL Sweep_Attempts in browsers that hold the Participant's Study_Identity, once those browsers' Sync_Queues hold no pending write, the Backend SHALL hold exactly one Study_Record of criterion 8 for each Sweep_Attempt that ended with the outcome Won or Lost and whose Sweep_Day is one of the five Sweep_Days of the Participant's Retention_Window, and none for any other Sweep_Attempt, including an abandoned Sweep_Attempt, a Sweep_Attempt of a Participant with no Retention_Window, and a Sweep_Attempt whose Sweep_Day is the Survey_Submission_Day, an earlier Sweep_Day, or the sixth or a later Sweep_Day after the Survey_Submission_Day (window property).
