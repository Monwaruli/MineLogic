### Requirement 20: Accounts, Guest Play, and Progress Sync

**User Story:** As a casual player on a short break, I want to play without signing up and keep my progress if I sign up later, so that MineLogic costs me nothing to try.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE MineLogic client SHALL let a Guest open and use the Hub, Onboarding_Flow, Tutorial_Player, Video_Panel, Mastery_Checkpoint, Practice_Mode, Timed_Game, Replay_Viewer, and Daily_Sweep, and read every leaderboard, without signing in, creating an Account, or entering an email address, with the same controls and results as a signed-in person except for where progress is stored (criterion 2) and the leaderboard differences of Requirement 19 criteria 10 to 12, and SHALL never make a Guest answer or dismiss a request to sign in before continuing.
2. WHILE a person is a Guest, WHEN a Progress_Record is recorded for the person outside a Study_Session, THE Progress_Service SHALL store the record in the browser's local storage as part of the Guest_Progress before displaying any effect of it, such as a completion indicator, a game summary, or a changed Mastery_Bar, so that the record remains after page reloads, closed tabs, and browser restarts until criterion 6 moves it or the browser's site data is cleared.
3. THE Auth_Service SHALL let a person create an Account with an email address of at most 254 characters in the form local-part@domain and a password of 8 to 72 characters, with at most one Account per email address compared without regard to letter case; sign in with that email address and password and stay signed in on that browser across page reloads and browser restarts until signing out or until the Auth_Service ends the sign-in session; sign out; and request a password reset email whose link works only once, expires 3,600 seconds after it is sent, and sets a new password of 8 to 72 characters.
4. WHEN a person creates an Account, THE Auth_Service SHALL require a Display_Name of 3 to 20 characters, each an ASCII letter (A to Z or a to z), a digit (0 to 9), an underscore, or a hyphen, that matches no other Account's Display_Name when letter case is ignored.
5. IF a Display_Name submitted for a new Account breaks a rule of criterion 4, including by matching the Display_Name of an Account created at the same moment, THEN THE Backend SHALL create no Account, store no Display_Name, and return the first broken rule in the order length, allowed characters, uniqueness.
6. WHEN an Account becomes signed in on a browser, through sign-in or Account creation, while that browser's Guest_Progress holds at least one Progress_Record, THE Progress_Service SHALL merge the Guest_Progress into the Account as the Progress_Union of the Account's Progress_Records and the Guest_Progress by moving every Progress_Record of the Guest_Progress into the Sync_Queue as a write for that Account, after which the Guest_Progress is empty, each moved record belongs to that Account only, and no moved game result is submitted to the Leaderboard_Service. The Daily_Streak and longest Daily_Streak are then computed from the merged set of completed Sweep_Days, so a Guest with three consecutive completed Sweep_Days and an Account with the next three has a longest Daily_Streak of 6 after the merge.
7. FOR ALL sets A, B, and C of Progress_Records, the Progress_Union of A and B SHALL equal the Progress_Union of B and A (confluence property); the Progress_Union of C with the Progress_Union of A and B SHALL equal the Progress_Union of A with the Progress_Union of B and C (associativity property); the Progress_Union of B with the Progress_Union of A and B SHALL equal the Progress_Union of A and B (idempotence property); and the Progress_Union of A and B SHALL hold every record of A and of B and no other record, except that a Bank_History entry can be replaced by a later entry for the same Board identifier (preservation property).
8. WHILE a person is signed in to an Account, WHEN a Progress_Record is recorded for the person outside a Study_Session, THE Progress_Service SHALL add the record to the Sync_Queue as a write for that Account before displaying any effect of it, the Sync_Queue SHALL hold the write in the browser's local storage until the Backend confirms it or criterion 13 drops it, so that the Account's Progress_Records in the Backend are the Progress_Union of every record delivered for that Account, and THE Progress_Service SHALL use as the person's progress the Progress_Union of the Account's Progress_Records loaded from the Backend (at sign-in and at each page load) and the Account's writes waiting in the Sync_Queue.
9. IF a Backend write fails because the Backend could not be reached (no response within 10 seconds of sending, no network connection, or a response that the Backend is temporarily unavailable), or because the Leaderboard_Service rejects it under Requirement 19 criterion 8, THEN THE Sync_Queue SHALL keep the write in the browser's local storage, including across page reloads and browser restarts, and send it again: after an unreachable Backend, 5 seconds after the failure, then after delays that double each time up to at most 300 seconds, with no limit on the number of attempts, and also at once when a MineLogic page loads or the browser regains a network connection; after a rate-limit rejection, 3,600 seconds after the rejection or at the first page load after that time. THE Sync_Queue SHALL display the notice that syncing is paused (Requirement 28.5) until no write is waiting because the Backend could not be reached.
10. FOR ALL sequences of writes queued for one Account or for one Participant, THE Sync_Queue SHALL send the writes one at a time in queue order, each only after every earlier write of the sequence is confirmed by the Backend or dropped under criterion 13 (except that a write waiting out a rate-limit rejection holds back no later write), and within 2 seconds of the write becoming the next write of its sequence while the Backend can be reached and, for an Account's write, while that Account is signed in on the browser (ordering property). FOR ALL writes held by the Sync_Queue, including Study_Records and leaderboard results, delivering the same write more than once, including after its confirmation was lost, SHALL leave the Backend in the same state as delivering it once, and each repeat of a confirmed write SHALL be confirmed again (idempotence property).
11. WHEN a person signs out, or the Auth_Service ends the person's sign-in session, THE MineLogic client SHALL, within 1 second on the Reference_Device, stop displaying the Account's Display_Name and leaderboard ranks, use the browser's Guest_Progress as the person's progress in place of the Account's Progress_Records, and keep the Account's writes waiting in the Sync_Queue unsent until that Account is next signed in on that browser, while still sending writes for Participants.
12. IF the Auth_Service or the Backend refuses an Account creation, a sign-in, or a password reset, or gives no response within 10 seconds of the request, THEN THE MineLogic client SHALL display an error message that states either the returned reason or that the service cannot be reached (for a refused sign-in, only that the email address or password is incorrect, without saying which), keep the entered email address and Display_Name in the form, and leave the person's sign-in state, the Guest_Progress, and the Sync_Queue unchanged.
13. IF the Backend rejects a write for any reason other than the rate limit of Requirement 19 criterion 8, an ended sign-in session, or the withdrawal of the write's Participant, THEN THE Sync_Queue SHALL remove the write from the queue without sending it again, keep a copy of it in the browser's local storage that no longer counts as the person's progress, and display a notice that names the kind of record (such as a milestone, a game result, or a leaderboard result) and the reason the Backend returned. A Study_Record write rejected because its Participant withdrew SHALL be deleted from the browser with no copy and no notice.
14. IF the browser's local storage is full or unavailable when the Progress_Service or the Sync_Queue tries to store a record, THEN THE MineLogic client SHALL display, within 1 second on the Reference_Device, a notice that progress cannot be saved in this browser, which stays until the person dismisses it; keep every record already stored unchanged; keep the current screen and any Active_Game usable; use the unsaved record as the person's progress until the page is closed or reloaded; and, while an Account is signed in, still send the record to the Backend under criteria 9 and 10 while the page stays open.
