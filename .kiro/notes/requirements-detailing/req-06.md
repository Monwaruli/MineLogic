### Requirement 6: Board Supply and Offline Puzzle Bank

**User Story:** As the developer, I want a pre-computed bank of no-guess boards as a fallback, so that Expert play stays smooth on slower computers.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN a Timed_Game or a Practice_Mode game without an Assigned_Fixed_Board is requested at a Difficulty, THE Board_Provider SHALL supply exactly one Board for that game: a No_Guess_Board with that Difficulty's width, height, and mine count, produced by the Board_Generator or, under criterion 2, taken from the Puzzle_Bank.
2. WHERE the Puzzle_Bank is enabled for a Difficulty, IF the Board_Generator has not produced a Board within that Difficulty's time limit from Requirement 5 (500 milliseconds for Beginner, 1,000 milliseconds for Intermediate, 3,000 milliseconds for Expert), measured on the player's computer from the game request, THEN THE Board_Provider SHALL request a Puzzle_Bank Board of that Difficulty while the generation continues, supply whichever of the two Boards is ready first, and stop the generation if the Puzzle_Bank Board is supplied.
3. WHERE the Puzzle_Bank is enabled for a Difficulty, THE Board_Provider SHALL supply from the Puzzle_Bank only a Board of that Difficulty that is not in the player's Bank_History, or, if every Puzzle_Bank Board of that Difficulty is already in the player's Bank_History, the Board of that Difficulty that was supplied to the player least recently.
4. WHERE the Puzzle_Bank is enabled for a Difficulty, IF the Puzzle_Bank cannot be reached, holds no Board of that Difficulty, or returns a Board that the Board_Parser rejects or whose width, height, or mine count differs from that Difficulty's, THEN THE Board_Provider SHALL supply no Puzzle_Bank Board for that game request, keep the client-side generation running with the progress indicator from Requirement 5 displayed, and supply the generated Board if the Board_Generator produces it before the 30-second limit of criterion 8, without showing an error message about the Puzzle_Bank.
5. WHEN the Offline_Generator is run, THE Offline_Generator SHALL write, for each Difficulty, exactly 1,000 Boards as Board_Format text that the Board_Parser accepts, where each Board is a No_Guess_Board with that Difficulty's width, height, and mine count, has an identifier that no other Board in the output shares, and has a set of mine positions that no other Board of that Difficulty in the output shares.
6. WHEN Board_Format text is imported into the Puzzle_Bank, THE Bank_Importer SHALL load only the Boards that the Board_Parser accepts, that have the width, height, and mine count of one Difficulty, that the Solver verifies as No_Guess_Boards with no Inexact_State on the Canonical_Solve_Path, whose identifier and set of mine positions match no Board already in the Puzzle_Bank or earlier in the same import, and whose set of mine positions matches no Fixed_Board's mine positions as placed, rotated, or reflected, and SHALL report the number of Boards loaded and, for each rejected Board, its identifier (or its line number when the identifier cannot be read) and the name of the first check it failed, in the order listed.
7. WHEN a game with an Assigned_Fixed_Board is requested, THE Board_Provider SHALL supply that Assigned_Fixed_Board unchanged and SHALL NOT substitute a Board from the Board_Generator or the Puzzle_Bank, including when the Assigned_Fixed_Board cannot be loaded.
8. IF no Board has been supplied within 30 seconds of a game request, or the game's Assigned_Fixed_Board cannot be loaded because the Backend request fails or the Board_Parser rejects its text, THEN THE MineLogic client SHALL end that game request without a Board, display an error message indicating that no Board could be prepared in place of any progress indicator, offer a control that repeats the game request, and record no Move_Log or game result for that request.
9. FOR ALL Boards in the Puzzle_Bank and FOR ALL Board_States on those Boards in which the Start_Tile is revealed, no mine is revealed, and at least one non-mine Tile is covered, the Solver SHALL classify at least one covered Tile as Provably_Safe (always-a-safe-move property, matching Requirement 5 for Board_Generator output).
