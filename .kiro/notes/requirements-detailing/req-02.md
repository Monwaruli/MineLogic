### Requirement 2: Board Input and Display

**User Story:** As a learner using a mouse or a trackpad, I want every common Minesweeper input to work and the board to respond at once, so that my input device does not hold me back.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN the player presses the primary button, with no other button held, on a covered, unflagged Tile, THE Board_View SHALL issue one Reveal_Click for that Tile at the moment of the press, without waiting for the button's release.
2. WHEN the player presses the secondary button, with no other button held, on a covered Tile, THE Board_View SHALL issue one Flag_Toggle for that Tile at the moment of the press, whether or not the Tile is flagged.
3. WHEN the player, on a revealed Tile (including a zero-Number Tile), presses the primary button or the middle button with no other button held, or presses the second of the primary and secondary buttons while the first is still held in a Press_Gesture that has not yet issued a Player_Action, THE Board_View SHALL issue one Chord_Click for that Tile at the moment of the press, whether or not the count of flagged Neighbors equals the Number.
4. WHEN the Board_Engine applies a Player_Action, THE Board_View SHALL display the complete resulting Board_State, including every Tile revealed by a Cascade, the updated mine counter, and any end-of-game mine and flag markings, within 50 milliseconds of the browser input event that produced the Player_Action (or, for an action held until a Solver classification is ready, within 50 milliseconds of that classification), measured to the first displayed frame that shows the change, on the Reference_Device for every Board up to Expert size (30 columns by 16 rows).
5. WHILE the browser viewport is at least 1280 by 720 CSS pixels, THE Board_View SHALL display every Tile of a Board up to Expert size, the mine counter, and the game clock when one is shown, without page or board scrolling, with every Tile drawn at least 24 by 24 CSS pixels.
6. THE Board_View SHALL draw every Overlay with the border pattern of its role (interacting Number, safe Tile, or mine), which no other Overlay role uses, and with a text or icon label that names the role, in addition to the role's color, so that the role of every Overlay can be identified when the board is displayed in grayscale.
7. THE Board_View SHALL draw every Number from 1 to 8 fully visible and with a contrast ratio, as defined in WCAG 2.1, of at least 3:1 against the background directly behind the Number, including while an Overlay or an Overlay label is drawn on the Tile.
8. THE Board_View SHALL prevent the browser context menu from opening and the browser's middle-button scroll mode from starting during every Press_Gesture that starts inside the board area, including when a button is released outside the board area, and SHALL leave both browser behaviors unchanged for Press_Gestures that start outside the board area.
9. WHILE the browser viewport is narrower than 1280 CSS pixels or shorter than 720 CSS pixels, THE Board_View SHALL keep every Tile at least 24 by 24 CSS pixels and SHALL make every Tile, the mine counter, and the game clock when one is shown reachable by scrolling.
10. FOR ALL sequences of button presses and releases on a board, the Board_View SHALL issue at most one Player_Action per Press_Gesture, and SHALL issue no Player_Action for a press that matches none of criteria 1 to 3 (such as a secondary press alone on a revealed Tile, a primary press on a flagged Tile, a middle press on a covered Tile, or a press outside every Tile) or that is made after the board's game has ended (input-mapping property).
