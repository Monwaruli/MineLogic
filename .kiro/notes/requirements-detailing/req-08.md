### Requirement 8: Game Metric Definitions

**User Story:** As a learner and as the researcher, I want precise definitions of Logical Move Rate, Pattern Recognition Speed, and Chording Efficiency, so that the numbers separate deductive skill from luck and hardware.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN a Timed_Game ends with the outcome Won, Lost, Timed_Out, or Interrupted, THE Metrics_Engine SHALL compute the Game_Metrics from only the Board and the game's Move_Log, taking the outcome and the elapsed milliseconds from the End_Entry, including when the Move_Log holds no Player_Action.
2. THE Metrics_Engine SHALL classify each Counted_Reveal_Action as a Logical_Move when the Solver classifies every one of its Target_Tiles as Provably_Safe in the Board_State immediately before the action and as a Guess otherwise, SHALL classify each Flag_Toggle that places a flag, made in a Board_State that is not an Inexact_State, as a Wrong_Flag when its Tile is not Provably_Mine in that Board_State, and SHALL leave the Start_Tile reveal and every Reveal_Action taken in an Inexact_State unclassified.
3. THE Metrics_Engine SHALL compute Logical_Move_Rate as 100 times the number of Logical_Moves among the Counted_Reveal_Actions divided by the number of Counted_Reveal_Actions, rounded to one decimal place from the exact quotient with exact halves rounded up (for example, 1 Logical_Move among 16 Counted_Reveal_Actions gives 6.3).
4. IF a game has no Counted_Reveal_Actions, THEN THE Metrics_Engine SHALL report Logical_Move_Rate as not available rather than as a number.
5. WHEN a Timed_Game ends with the outcome Won, THE Metrics_Engine SHALL compute Chording_Efficiency as Total_Clicks divided by the Board's 3BV, rounded to two decimal places from the exact quotient with exact halves rounded up (for example, 49 Total_Clicks on a 3BV of 40 gives 1.23), where Total_Clicks is the number of Player_Actions in the Move_Log, including Wasted_Clicks and excluding the End_Entry.
6. FOR ALL Well_Formed_Boards with a Start_Tile, revealing the Start_Tile, then one zero-Number Tile in each other Opening, then each still-covered non-mine Tile, with one Reveal_Click per Tile and no Flag_Toggle, SHALL win the game with exactly 3BV Reveal_Clicks and a reported Chording_Efficiency of 1.00 (3BV model property), and FOR ALL won games whose Move_Log contains no Chord_Click, Total_Clicks SHALL be at least the Board's 3BV and Chording_Efficiency SHALL be at least 1.00 (no-chord lower-bound property).
7. THE Metrics_Engine SHALL compute Pattern_Recognition_Speed for each of the eight Pattern_Types as the median of resolution time minus appearance time over that type's resolved Pattern_Instances that are neither excluded nor superseded, taking the mean of the two middle values when the count is even, converted from milliseconds to seconds and rounded to one decimal place from the exact value with exact halves rounded up (for example, durations of 1,200 and 1,300 milliseconds give 1.3).
8. IF a Pattern_Type has no resolved Pattern_Instance that is neither excluded nor superseded, THEN THE Metrics_Engine SHALL report that Pattern_Type's Pattern_Recognition_Speed as not available rather than as a number.
9. THE Metrics_Engine SHALL report, for each of the eight Pattern_Types, the counts of superseded, excluded, resolved, and unresolved Pattern_Instances, counting each Pattern_Instance in exactly one category: superseded if marked superseded, otherwise excluded if marked excluded, otherwise resolved if it has a resolution time, and otherwise unresolved.
10. THE Metrics_Engine SHALL report for each game the Board's 3BV, Total_Clicks, the counts of Reveal_Clicks, Flag_Toggles, and Chord_Clicks (which sum to Total_Clicks), the count of Wasted_Clicks, the Guess count as the number of Counted_Reveal_Actions classified as Guesses, and the Wrong_Flag count as the number of Flag_Toggles classified as Wrong_Flags, counting each placement separately, including flags later removed.
11. IF the Board_State immediately before any Player_Action after the Start_Tile reveal is an Inexact_State, THEN THE Metrics_Engine SHALL mark the Game_Metrics as partial and report the number of Reveal_Actions excluded from the Counted_Reveal_Actions and the number of flag-placing Flag_Toggles left unclassified for that reason.
12. FOR ALL games with at least one Counted_Reveal_Action, the reported Logical_Move_Rate SHALL lie between 0.0 and 100.0 inclusive, SHALL equal 100.0 exactly when every Counted_Reveal_Action is a Logical_Move, and SHALL equal 0.0 exactly when no Counted_Reveal_Action is a Logical_Move (bounds and exactness property).
13. THE Metrics_Engine SHALL label every set of Game_Metrics with the Metrics_Version that produced the Game_Metrics.
14. IF a Timed_Game ends with an outcome other than Won, THEN THE Metrics_Engine SHALL report Chording_Efficiency as not available rather than as a number and SHALL still report Total_Clicks and the Board's 3BV.
15. IF the Board has no Start_Tile, the Move_Log's Board identifier differs from the Board's identifier, the Move_Log has no End_Entry, or replaying the Move_Log on the Board with the Board_Engine finds an entry that the Board_Engine would not have logged (such as a first Player_Action other than the Reveal_Click on the Start_Tile at 0 milliseconds, or a Player_Action after a winning or losing action), a Wasted_Click mark that does not match whether the replayed action changed the Board_State, or an End_Entry that contradicts the replay (Won or Lost when the last Player_Action did not produce that outcome, Timed_Out or Interrupted after a winning or losing action, or a time earlier than the last Player_Action's time or, for Won and Lost, later than it), THEN THE Metrics_Engine SHALL return an error that names the failed check and, for an entry-level failure, the index of the first offending entry, and SHALL return no Game_Metrics.
16. FOR ALL Boards and Move_Logs, adding any delay to the wall-clock time that each Solver classification takes SHALL leave the Game_Metrics, including the partial mark, unchanged (device-independence property).
