### Requirement 4: Constraint Satisfaction Solver

**User Story:** As a learner, I want MineLogic to know exactly which tiles can be proven safe, so that hints, metrics, and board guarantees are based on real logic rather than luck.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN the Solver receives a Board_State that is at most 30 Tiles wide and 30 Tiles high and has at least one Consistent_Configuration, THE Solver SHALL classify every covered Tile, including flagged Tiles and Tiles that border no revealed Number, as exactly one of Provably_Safe, Provably_Mine, or Undetermined according to the Consistent_Configuration definition, using only the revealed Numbers and the Board's total mine count, and SHALL return no classification for revealed Tiles.
2. FOR ALL Board_States and FOR ALL sets of covered Tiles, including the empty set, sets with more Tiles than the Board's mine count, and sets that contain Provably_Safe Tiles, replacing the Board_State's flagged Tiles with that set SHALL leave the Solver's result unchanged (flag-invariance property).
3. FOR ALL Board_States with at most 20 covered Tiles, the Solver SHALL finish without marking the Board_State as an Inexact_State, SHALL return an error indicating that the Board_State is inconsistent exactly when no mine placement on the covered Tiles is a Consistent_Configuration, and SHALL otherwise return the classification obtained by enumerating every mine placement on the covered Tiles and keeping only the Consistent_Configurations (model-based property).
4. FOR ALL Boards and FOR ALL Board_States reachable on a Board through Player_Actions that reveal no mine, including Board_States that the Solver marks as Inexact_States, every Tile the Solver classifies as Provably_Safe SHALL be a non-mine Tile of the Board, and every Tile the Solver classifies as Provably_Mine SHALL be a mine of the Board (soundness property).
5. FOR ALL pairs of Board_States on the same Board where the second reveals every Tile the first reveals plus further non-mine Tiles and the Solver does not mark the second as an Inexact_State, every Tile the Solver classifies as Provably_Safe in the first that is still covered in the second SHALL be classified as Provably_Safe in the second, and every Tile the Solver classifies as Provably_Mine in the first SHALL be classified as Provably_Mine in the second (monotonicity property).
6. WHEN the Solver classifies every Board_State on the Canonical_Solve_Paths of 20 Expert No_Guess_Boards that the Board_Generator produces from 20 fixed seeds recorded in the test suite, THE Solver SHALL return the classification of at least 95% of those Board_States within 100 milliseconds of receiving each Board_State, on the Reference_Device.
7. IF the Solver uses its whole Step_Budget before finishing the classification of a Board_State, THEN THE Solver SHALL stop classifying, return every classification already proven, return every other covered Tile as Undetermined, and mark the Board_State as an Inexact_State.
8. WHILE the Solver is classifying a Board_State, THE Board_View SHALL display the result of each Player_Action whose result does not depend on that classification within the 50 milliseconds set by Requirement 2, and SHALL display every other screen update that does not depend on that classification, such as a game clock tick, within 100 milliseconds of when the update is due, on the Reference_Device.
9. IF the Solver receives a Board_State that has no Consistent_Configuration, such as a Board_State in which a revealed Number is greater than its count of covered Neighbors or the Board's total mine count is greater than the number of covered Tiles, THEN THE Solver SHALL return an error indicating that the Board_State is inconsistent instead of a classification, unless the Step_Budget in criterion 7 is used up first.
10. FOR ALL Board_States and FOR ALL rotations by 90, 180, or 270 degrees and reflections across the horizontal or vertical axis, the Solver's result for the transformed Board_State SHALL equal the same transformation applied to the Solver's result for the original Board_State, whenever the Solver marks neither Board_State as an Inexact_State (symmetry property).
11. FOR ALL Board_States, two classifications with the same Step_Budget SHALL return the same result, including the same Inexact_State mark, regardless of device speed, CPU load, or the wall-clock time each classification takes (determinism property).
12. WHEN the Solver runs on the Reference_Device, THE Solver SHALL use up its Step_Budget, when it does, within 1,000 milliseconds of receiving the Board_State.
