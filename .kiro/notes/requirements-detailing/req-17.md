### Requirement 17: Mastery Progress and the Hub

**User Story:** As a learner, I want to see my mastery of each difficulty and what to do next, so that I can make progress in short breaks without planning my own curriculum.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHILE a Difficulty's Mastery_Checkpoint is recorded as passed (Requirement 13.4), THE Progress_Service SHALL compute that Difficulty's Mastery_Bar as 100%, whatever that Difficulty's Skill_Check results, its number of completed Lessons, and the results of later Checkpoint_Attempts.
2. WHILE a Difficulty's Mastery_Checkpoint is not recorded as passed, THE Progress_Service SHALL compute that Difficulty's Mastery_Bar, without rounding, as the lesser of 90% and the sum of a Skill_Check part and a Lesson part. The Skill_Check part is 10% when at least one recorded Skill_Check result for that Difficulty is Correct and 0% otherwise. The Lesson part is 80% times the number of that Difficulty's Lessons recorded as complete, each counted once, divided by the number of Lessons the Tutorial_Player provides for that Difficulty.
3. FOR ALL progress records (sets of milestones, game results, and completed Sweep_Days) and FOR ALL additions of one milestone, game result, or completed Sweep_Day to them, including a Trap or Other Skill_Check result for a Difficulty that already has a Correct result, every Difficulty's Mastery_Bar after the addition SHALL be equal to or higher than before (monotonicity property), and every Mastery_Bar SHALL depend only on the set of distinct records, so that recording order, repeated records, and the order of a merge (Requirement 20.6) leave it unchanged (order-independence property).
4. WHILE the Hub is shown, THE Hub SHALL display the Mastery_Bar of each Difficulty, in the order Beginner, Intermediate, Expert, as a bar filled in proportion to its value and labeled with that value as a whole-number percentage rounded down; the Daily_Streak for the Current_Sweep_Day and the longest Daily_Streak (Requirement 18), each as a whole number of Sweep_Days and shown even when it is 0; and the leaderboards of Requirement 19.10.
5. WHILE the Hub is shown, THE Hub SHALL display each Difficulty's four Loop_Steps in Loop_Step order, each with a text or icon label, in addition to any color, that states whether the Loop_Step is complete. The Lessons Loop_Step is complete when every Lesson of that Difficulty is recorded as complete. The Mastery_Checkpoint Loop_Step is complete when that Difficulty's Mastery_Checkpoint is recorded as passed. The Practice_Mode Loop_Step is complete when at least one Practice_Mode board of that Difficulty is recorded as completed (Requirement 14.11). The Timed_Game Loop_Step is complete when at least one Timed_Game at that Difficulty, other than a Daily_Sweep, Pre_Test, or Post_Test game, is stored with the outcome Won (Requirement 15.8).
6. WHILE the Hub is shown and at least one Loop_Step is incomplete, THE Hub SHALL display exactly one recommendation, with a control that opens it as criterion 7 describes. The recommendation names the target Difficulty and that Difficulty's first incomplete Loop_Step in Loop_Step order, and, for the Lessons Loop_Step, that Difficulty's first Lesson, in Requirement 11.1 order, that is not recorded as complete. The target Difficulty is the lowest Difficulty (Beginner, then Intermediate, then Expert) that has an incomplete Loop_Step and has no Skill_Check result recorded as Correct, or, when no Difficulty meets both conditions, the lowest Difficulty that has an incomplete Loop_Step, so that the learner finishes the whole loop of one Difficulty before the recommendation moves on.
7. WHILE the Hub is shown, THE Hub SHALL display an enabled control for every Loop_Step of every Difficulty, including Loop_Steps that are complete, whatever the Mastery_Bars, Skill_Check results, Loop_Step statuses, and recommendation. The Lessons control opens the Tutorial_Player at that Difficulty's first Lesson that is not recorded as complete, or at its first Lesson when every Lesson is complete. The Mastery_Checkpoint control starts a new Checkpoint_Attempt (Requirement 13.1). The Practice_Mode control starts Practice_Mode (Requirement 14.1). The Timed_Game control starts a Timed_Game (Requirement 15.1).
8. WHEN the Hub is opened, a new Sweep_Day begins while the Hub is shown, or a signed-in person's Account progress finishes loading from the Backend while the Hub is shown, THE Hub SHALL, within 1 second on the Reference_Device, display the Mastery_Bars, streaks, Loop_Step statuses, and recommendation of criteria 4 to 6, computed from the Current_Sweep_Day, from every write still waiting in the Sync_Queue, and from the person's stored progress loaded so far (Requirement 20).
9. IF loading a signed-in person's Account progress from the Backend fails, or has not finished 5 seconds after the Hub is opened, THEN THE Hub SHALL, within 1 second on the Reference_Device, display the notice that syncing is paused (Requirement 28.5), keep displaying what criterion 8 computed, and keep every control of criterion 7 enabled.
10. WHILE the Hub is shown and every Loop_Step of every Difficulty is complete, THE Hub SHALL display no recommendation.
