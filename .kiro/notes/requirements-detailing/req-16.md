### Requirement 16: Post-Game Instant Replay

**User Story:** As a learner who just lost, I want to see the safe tiles and patterns I missed, so that I reflect on my reasoning instead of blaming luck.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN the Metrics_Engine returns the Game_Metrics of a Timed_Game other than a Pre_Test or Post_Test game that ended with the outcome Won or Lost, THE Timed_Game SHALL display on the game's summary, together with the Game_Metrics, a replay control whose selection opens the Replay_Viewer for that game's Board and Move_Log within 500 milliseconds of the selection on the Reference_Device. The Replay_Viewer SHALL open at Replay_Position 0 for a won game and at the next-to-last Replay_Position for a lost game. It SHALL use only the Board, the Move_Log, and the results of that Metrics_Engine run held in the browser, so that the replay also opens when the Backend cannot be reached. After a Pre_Test or Post_Test game, the Timed_Game SHALL display no replay control (Requirement 22.11).
2. THE Replay_Viewer SHALL provide a play control, a pause control, step-forward and step-back controls, and a jump control. The play control SHALL advance the Replay_Position by one every 1,000 milliseconds, starting 1,000 milliseconds after play is selected, and SHALL stop at the last Replay_Position. The pause control SHALL stop the advance at the current Replay_Position. The step-forward and step-back controls SHALL change the Replay_Position by exactly one. The jump control SHALL move directly to any Replay_Position from 0 to the last that the learner chooses. Play and step forward SHALL be disabled at the last Replay_Position, step back SHALL be disabled at Replay_Position 0, and step forward, step back, and jump SHALL each pause playback. THE Replay_Viewer SHALL display the Board_State of each new Replay_Position within 100 milliseconds of the control input or scheduled advance on the Reference_Device.
3. WHILE the Replay_Viewer is at a Replay_Position other than 0, THE Replay_Viewer SHALL mark the Tile recorded for the current action with a current-action marker that can be told apart from every Overlay in grayscale, and SHALL label the current action as exactly one of the following, taking each label from the game's Metrics_Engine run (Requirement 9.1) and the Move_Log without classifying any action itself: the Start_Tile reveal; a Logical_Move or a Guess (a Counted_Reveal_Action, as the Metrics_Engine classified it); unclassified (a Reveal_Action taken in an Inexact_State); a Flag_Toggle; or a Wasted_Click (a Chord_Click whose Move_Log entry has the Wasted_Click mark).
4. WHEN the replay of a lost game opens, or the learner moves that replay to its next-to-last Replay_Position by play, step forward, step back, or jump, THE Replay_Viewer SHALL show that Replay_Position, which holds the Board_State immediately before the losing action (the game's last Player_Action), and SHALL draw an Overlay with the safe-Tile role (Requirement 2.6) on every covered Tile, flagged or not, that the Solver classified as Provably_Safe in that Board_State, and on no other Tile. Each such Overlay SHALL carry a label naming the Pattern_Type that the Pattern_Detector gave the Tile after the last Reveal_Action before the losing action, as the full name or an abbreviation that an on-screen legend maps to the full name. THE Replay_Viewer SHALL take the classification and the Pattern_Types from the game's Metrics_Engine run (Requirement 9.1), and SHALL keep these Overlays until the Replay_Position changes or a Pattern_Instance is selected.
5. WHEN the replay of a lost game opens, THE Replay_Viewer SHALL display, at every Replay_Position until the Replay_Viewer closes, a list of every Pattern_Instance whose Pattern_Instance_Status in the game's Metrics_Engine run is unresolved, and of no other Pattern_Instance. Each entry SHALL show the Pattern_Instance's Pattern_Type and its appearance time in whole seconds of elapsed game time, rounded down (for example, 42,999 milliseconds shows as 42 seconds). Entries SHALL be ordered by appearance time, earliest first, then by each Pattern_Instance's first Tile in row-major order. In place of an empty list, THE Replay_Viewer SHALL display a message that no Pattern_Instance was left unresolved.
6. WHEN the learner selects a listed Pattern_Instance, THE Replay_Viewer SHALL pause playback, show the Replay_Position of the Reveal_Action at which the Pattern_Instance appeared, remove every other Overlay and highlight, and draw an Overlay with the interacting-Number role on each of the Pattern_Instance's Anchor_Numbers and an Overlay with the safe-Tile role on each of its Tiles (Requirement 2.6). When the Pattern_Type is Mine_Count_Endgame, it SHALL also highlight the mine counter. THE Replay_Viewer SHALL take the Anchor_Numbers and Tiles from the game's Metrics_Engine run and keep these highlights until the Replay_Position changes or another Pattern_Instance is selected.
7. IF the Board_State immediately before the losing action of a lost game is an Inexact_State, THEN THE Replay_Viewer SHALL display a notice that the Solver did not finish classifying that Board_State, so Tiles without an Overlay may also have been safe. The notice SHALL appear together with the Overlays of criterion 4 and stay as long as they do, including when no Tile receives an Overlay.
8. FOR ALL Boards and Move_Logs of Timed_Games that ended with the outcome Won or Lost, and FOR ALL sequences of play, pause, step-forward, step-back, jump, and Pattern_Instance selection inputs, the Replay_Viewer SHALL show at each Replay_Position k exactly the Board_State that the Board_Engine produces by replaying the first k Player_Actions of the Move_Log on the Board, whatever sequence of inputs reached k; SHALL show no mine before the last Replay_Position and the end-of-game mine and flag markings of Requirement 1 at the last Replay_Position; SHALL issue no Player_Action; and SHALL leave the game's Move_Log, Game_Metrics, and stored result unchanged (replay fidelity and path-independence property).
9. FOR ALL Boards and Move_Logs of Timed_Games that ended with the outcome Won or Lost, the Replay_Viewer SHALL give each Player_Action exactly one label and exactly one Start_Tile reveal label, and the number of Guess labels SHALL equal the Guess count, the number of Wasted_Click labels the Wasted_Click count, the number of Flag_Toggle labels the Flag_Toggle count, and the number of unclassified labels the count of Reveal_Actions excluded for Inexact_States (0 when the Game_Metrics are not partial). For a lost game, the Replay_Viewer SHALL list, for each Pattern_Type, exactly as many Pattern_Instances as that Pattern_Type's unresolved count in the Game_Metrics (consistency property).
10. IF the Metrics_Engine returns an error instead of Game_Metrics for a Timed_Game that is not a Pre_Test or Post_Test game, THEN THE Timed_Game SHALL display no replay control and SHALL display on the game's summary a message that the replay is not available for that game.
