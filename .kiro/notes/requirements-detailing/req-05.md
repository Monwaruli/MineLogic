### Requirement 5: No-Guess Board Generation

**User Story:** As a learner, I want every timed and practice board to be solvable by logic alone, so that good reasoning is never punished by a forced 50/50 guess.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN a full Board is requested for a Difficulty, THE Board_Generator SHALL output a Board that has that Difficulty's width, height, and mine count (width 9, height 9, and 10 mines for Beginner; 16, 16, and 40 for Intermediate; 30, 16, and 99 for Expert), has a Start_Tile that is not a mine and has no mine among its Neighbors, and is accepted by the Board_Parser after the Board_Printer prints it.
2. THE Board_Generator SHALL output only No_Guess_Boards whose Canonical_Solve_Path, starting from the Board_State after the Start_Tile reveal and its Cascade, ends with every non-mine Tile revealed and contains no Inexact_State.
3. FOR ALL Difficulties and Generation_Seeds, and FOR ALL Board_States of the resulting Board that are not Inexact_States and in which the Start_Tile is revealed, no mine is revealed, and at least one non-mine Tile is covered, the Solver SHALL classify at least one covered Tile as Provably_Safe (always-a-safe-move property).
4. FOR ALL Difficulties and Generation_Seeds, two requests with the same Difficulty and Generation_Seed served by the same Generator_Version SHALL output Boards with identical width, height, mine positions, Start_Tile, identifier, and Board metadata, regardless of the device or supported browser that runs the Board_Generator, and each output Board's identifier SHALL differ from the identifier of every Fixed_Board, every Puzzle_Bank Board, and every other output Board that differs in width, height, mine positions, or Start_Tile (determinism and unique-identifier properties).
5. WHEN the Board_Generator outputs a Board, THE Board_Generator SHALL record in the Board's metadata, stored beside the Board_Format text, the Generation_Seed, the Generator_Version, the Board's 3BV, and, for every Pattern_Type, the count of Pattern_Instances that the Pattern_Detector reports as resolved on the Canonical_Solve_Path (Requirement 7 criterion 10), recording 0 for a Pattern_Type with no such Pattern_Instance.
6. WHEN 100 full Boards of one Difficulty are requested one after another without a Generation_Seed on the Reference_Device, THE Board_Generator SHALL output at least 90 of the 100 Boards within that Difficulty's time limit, measured from each request to the output of its Board: 500 milliseconds for Beginner, 1,000 milliseconds for Intermediate, and 3,000 milliseconds for Expert.
7. WHILE the Board_Generator is generating a Board, THE Board_View SHALL draw every screen update, including the visible response to each mouse or trackpad input, no later than 100 milliseconds after the input or the update's scheduled time on the Reference_Device, and no later than 50 milliseconds after a Player_Action as Requirement 2 criterion 4 requires.
8. WHILE a game is waiting for the Board_Generator to output the game's Board, THE Board_View SHALL display a progress indicator stating that a no-guess Board is being generated, from no later than 100 milliseconds after the Board request until the Board is displayed.
9. IF a full Board request names a Difficulty other than Beginner, Intermediate, or Expert, or gives a Generation_Seed that is not an integer from 0 to 4,294,967,295, THEN THE Board_Generator SHALL output no Board and return an error that names the invalid Difficulty or Generation_Seed.
10. WHEN a full Board is requested without a Generation_Seed, THE Board_Generator SHALL generate the Board from a Generation_Seed chosen uniformly at random from 0 to 4,294,967,295.
11. WHEN the learner leaves a game's screen, or the Board_Provider supplies the game a Puzzle_Bank Board, before the Board_Generator has output the Board requested for that game, THE Board_Generator SHALL stop generating that Board within 100 milliseconds on the Reference_Device and output no Board for that request.
